Matcha Is a Major Part of the Blank Street Menu

Blank Street has built a large part of its menu around matcha, with options that go well beyond a standard Matcha Latte.

Depending on availability, you may find drinks such as Blueberry Matcha, Strawberry Shortcake Matcha, Daydream Matcha, Cookies & Cream Matcha, or other seasonal flavors.

These drinks should not be treated as nutritionally interchangeable.

A plain matcha drink may contain relatively few ingredients, while a flavored matcha can include milk, syrups, sauces, fruit preparations, or other additions that raise the calorie content.

Use the calculator to compare the specific matcha drink , rather than applying one calorie number to every matcha on the menu.

Hot and Iced Drinks Can Have Different Calories

The same drink name does not always mean the same nutrition.

Blank Street’s standard recipes can use different amounts of milk or other ingredients depending on whether a drink is served hot or iced. Size can change those amounts again.

For example, a hot latte and an iced latte may have different calorie totals even before you make any customizations.

When searching the calculator, match all three details whenever possible:

Drink name + preparation + size

That gives you a much more useful result than searching only for “latte” or “matcha.”

House Lattes Add More Than Espresso and Milk

Signature drinks such as Pistachio Latte, Daydream Latte, Strawberries & Cream Latte, and other house creations include ingredients beyond a basic espresso-and-milk latte.

Flavor syrups, chocolate, nut-based flavors, sweeteners, or specialty ingredients can make their calorie totals noticeably different from a standard latte.

If you are comparing a house latte with a regular latte, look at the finished drinks as separate menu items rather than assuming the difference is only flavor.

Cold Brew Can Range From Simple to Rich

Plain Original Cold Brew has a very different nutrition profile from a Cold Brew Latte or Mocha Cold Brew.

Adding milk changes calories and macros, while chocolate or other flavor ingredients can increase the total further.

This makes cold brew a useful example of why the name of the coffee base alone does not tell you the nutrition of the finished drink.

Choose the complete preparation shown in the calculator.

A Pastry Can Add More Calories Than the Coffee

Blank Street also serves pastries and baked goods, including items such as croissants, pain au chocolat, cinnamon buns, and other bakery choices.

If you order food with your drink, calculate them together.

For example:

Americano + croissant

and

Flavored matcha + cinnamon bun

represent very different total orders even though both are simply “coffee shop breakfast” choices.

Use the meal builder to combine the drink and food instead of checking each calorie number in isolation.

How to Get the Most Useful Result

If you order more than one item, add everything to your meal total.
