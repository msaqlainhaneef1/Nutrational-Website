Taim Mediterranean Kitchen Nutrition Calculator

A meal at taïm is built in layers.

You can start with a warm pita or a plate, choose falafel, chicken shawarma, cauliflower shawarma, eggplant, or another available main, then add greens, rice, salads, hummus, feta, vegetables, spreads, and sauces.

Each layer changes the final nutrition.

Use the Taim Mediterranean Kitchen Nutrition Calculator above to build the meal you actually ordered instead of relying on one calorie number for every falafel pita or Mediterranean plate.

The First Choice Is Pita or Plate

The easiest way to understand taïm nutrition is to begin with the meal format.

A Pita uses the house-baked pita and hummus as part of the starting build.

A Plate lets you choose a base and build the rest of the meal around it.

That means a Chicken Shawarma Pita and a Chicken Shawarma Plate should never automatically share the same nutrition.

The protein may be similar, but the base and remaining ingredients are different.

Plates Begin With the Base

Current plate bases include choices such as:

Toasted cumin rice

Super greens

Romaine lettuce

and:

Couscous .

This choice can significantly change the calorie and carbohydrate total before the main protein or sauces are added.

Rice and couscous should therefore remain separate from lettuce and greens in the calculator.

Do not use one generic:

Taim Bowl Base

value.

Falafel Is Central to the Taim Menu

Falafel is one of taïm's defining foods.

Current ordering options distinguish:

Traditional Green Falafel

Harissa Falafel

and the option to:

mix both styles .

These should be separate selectable options.

Even when calorie differences are small, recipe ingredients and seasonings can differ, and the product name should reflect what the customer actually ordered.

The O.G. Falafel Pita Should Stay a Complete Signature Recipe

The O.G. Falafel Pita is not simply:

pita + falafel .

Its standard recipe includes the pita, falafel, hummus, fresh salad components, pickled cabbage, and tahini.

If NutritionSolver has verified nutrition for the complete standard recipe, use that as the starting total.

Do not add hummus and tahini again unless the customer orders extra portions.

Signature Pitas and Custom Pitas Need Different Logic

This distinction will make the Taim calculator much better.

For a signature pita , start with the published recipe.

For a custom pita , calculate:

Pita + Main + Salads + Toppings + Spreads + Sauces .

Do not force a customer who ordered a standard O.G. Falafel Pita to manually rebuild every ingredient.

At the same time, do not assume every custom falafel pita is identical to the O.G.

Chicken Shawarma Needs Its Own Main

Current taïm menus include Chicken Shawarma as a major main option.

It should remain independent from falafel because its protein, fat, sodium, and preparation are different.

A useful calculator should support:

Chicken Shawarma Pita

and:

Chicken Shawarma Plate

as different meal paths.

Then apply the actual base, toppings, and sauces selected.

Beef and Lamb Shawarma Is a Current Menu Priority

The current menu now prominently features Halal Beef/Lamb Shawarma .

It is described as slow-roasted beef and lamb seasoned with taïm's kofta-style spice blend.

This should be treated as a current first-class main rather than hiding it under a generic meat category.

Use:

Beef/Lamb Shawarma

as its own searchable nutrition item and builder component.

protein

Cauliflower Shawarma Is Not Just a Vegetable Side

Current taïm menus offer Cauliflower Shawarma as a main.

It uses cauliflower prepared with shawarma seasoning and should remain separate from:

plain cauliflower

or:

vegetable topping .

If the cauliflower is cooked with oil and seasoning, a generic raw-cauliflower nutrition reference would understate the finished restaurant portion.

Golden Eggplant Needs Preparation Context

Golden Eggplant is another current main option.

Eggplant itself is relatively low in calories, but restaurant preparation can add substantial oil.

The calculator should therefore use the actual taïm preparation when available.

Do not estimate Golden Eggplant from plain raw eggplant.

Hummus Is Both a Base Ingredient and an Extra

Hummus can appear inside a standard pita, as a spread in a custom meal, or as a separate dip.

Those serving contexts should not be confused.

A small sandwich spread should not share the nutrition of a pint-sized catering container.

The database should store:

Hummus, meal portion

separately from:

Hummus dip or catering portion .

Red Pepper Babaganoush Needs Its Own Recipe

Taim's current spreads include Red Pepper Babaganoush.

It contains more than eggplant alone.

The recipe combines smoked eggplant with roasted red peppers, tahini, and seasonings.

It should therefore remain its own prepared-food record rather than being calculated from plain roasted eggplant.

Tzatziki Is Different From Hummus and Babaganoush

Tzatziki uses a yogurt-based recipe with cucumber, herbs, lemon, and garlic.

Its protein, fat, carbohydrate, and allergen profile is different from hummus and babaganoush.

Keep all three spreads separate:

Hummus

Red Pepper Babaganoush

Tzatziki .

Extra Sauce Must Be Counted Separately

If a standard signature pita already includes tahini, the standard nutrition should already account for its normal portion.

If the customer orders:

extra tahini

that additional serving should be added separately.

This avoids both undercounting and double-counting.

Whipped Garlic Is Especially Important to Measure

Garlic itself does not necessarily contribute many calories.

A creamy whipped garlic sauce can be a completely different story because its recipe can contain oil or other calorie-dense ingredients.

Do not calculate Whipped Garlic from raw garlic nutrition.

Use the actual prepared restaurant sauce.

Fries Need Sauce Choice Too

Taim's hand-cut fries are served with a sauce choice such as:

Saffron aioli

Harissa ketchup

or:

Whipped garlic .

The fries and dipping sauce should ideally remain separate components.

This allows the customer to calculate:

Fries without sauce

or:

Fries + the amount of sauce actually used .

A full sauce cup should not automatically be assumed if the customer only uses part of it.

Falafel & Tahini Should Have Piece Count

The small-bites menu includes Falafel & Tahini.

This item needs an explicit falafel quantity.

A record should say something like:

Falafel & Tahini, X pieces

instead of:

Falafel & Tahini

with no portion context.

Piece count is necessary if NutritionSolver later allows someone to add only one or two extra falafel pieces to a meal.

Dips Need Pita Versus Pita Chips

Current dip orders can be served with:

Pita

or:

Pita chips .

Those are different sides.

The correct structure is:

Choose Dip → Choose Pita or Pita Chips .

Do not hardcode both into one nutrition total unless the menu item always includes one specific choice.

Pita Is Not Nutritionally Invisible

The pita can contribute a substantial amount of the final carbohydrate and calorie total.

This becomes particularly important when comparing:

Falafel Pita

with:

Falafel Plate on greens .

The main falafel may be similar, but the meal structure is not.

Users should be able to see how much of the meal comes from the pita versus the filling.

Rice and Couscous Should Never Be Combined

Toasted Cumin Rice and Couscous have different recipes.

Even if their calories happen to be similar in one serving, their ingredient profiles and portion weights may differ.

Use separate records and store the restaurant serving size.

Green Bases Need Actual Restaurant Portions

Super Greens and Romaine are lower-energy base choices, but they still need defined portions.

Do not assign a generic:

1 cup lettuce

value unless taïm's serving actually equals one cup.

For restaurant builders, the scoop used in the actual meal is more useful than a generic household serving.

Beverages Need Their Own Serving Sizes

Ginger Mint Lemonade

The house-made drinks need exact serving sizes because liquid calories can change considerably with cup volume.

That serving size should appear directly in its nutrition record.

Lemonade and Smoothie Should Not Share One Drink Category

A Date Lime Banana Smoothie is nutritionally different from lemonade.

Use distinct subcategories such as:

Smoothies

Canned Drinks

and:

This makes calories, sugar, and serving size much easier to compare.

Packaged Beverages Should Use Manufacturer Nutrition

Spindrift, Coca-Cola products, and other packaged beverages should use their current manufacturer label.

They should not be treated as taïm recipes.

Store:

brand

package size

nutrition-label source

separately from restaurant-prepared drinks.

Calculate Taim in the Order the Kitchen Builds It

For a Pita :

Pita → Main → Salads → Toppings → Spreads → Sauce

For a Plate :

Base → Main → Salads → Toppings → Spreads → Sauce

Then add:

Premium topping → Small bite → Fries → Dip → Drink .

For signature meals such as the O.G. Falafel Pita, begin with the complete standard recipe and only calculate modifications.

For a custom meal, calculate every selected component.

This approach keeps the calculator faithful to the way taïm actually serves its food and prevents the same falafel, hummus, pita, or sauce from being accidentally counted twice.
