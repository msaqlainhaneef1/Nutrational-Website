Goodcents Nutrition Calculator

Goodcents sandwiches are built around freshly sliced meats and cheeses, soft bread, vegetables, and your choice of dressings and condiments. The final nutrition can change considerably depending on the sub, portion size, cheese, toppings, sauces, and anything you add alongside it.

Use the Goodcents Nutrition Calculator above to compare subs and other menu items or combine everything you order into one complete meal.

Start With the Exact Goodcents Sub

Goodcents separates its cold sandwiches into Signature Subs and Classic Subs.

Signature choices combine several meats. Current examples include:

Goodcents Original with ham, bologna, salami, pepperoni, and cheese.

Penny Club with roast beef, turkey, ham, and cheese.

Italian with capicola, pepperoni, salami, and cheese.

Ultimate Club with ham, turkey, bacon, and cheese.

Classic Subs use simpler meat combinations such as turkey, ham, roast beef, chicken, tuna salad, salami, pepperoni, or a vegetarian build.

Because those recipes use different meats and amounts of cheese, they should have their own nutrition totals rather than sharing one generic sub value.

Sub Length Is One of the Biggest Variables

Goodcents sells sandwiches in different lengths, so the sub size needs to match the portion you actually order.

A larger sandwich contains more bread, meat, cheese, and toppings. As a result, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and sodium can all increase substantially.

When comparing two options, make sure you are comparing the same size.

For example:

Penny Club, smaller portion

is not directly comparable with:

Goodcents Original, whole sub

unless the serving sizes are taken into account.

If you eat half of a larger sandwich, calculate the portion you actually consume rather than automatically assigning yourself the nutrition for the entire sub.

More Meat Changes More Than Protein

Goodcents currently promotes its freshly sliced subs with more meat than previous formulations.

More meat can raise protein, but it can also increase calories, sodium, cholesterol, and fat depending on the sandwich.

The effect is especially noticeable on multi-meat subs such as the Original, Italian, Penny Club, and Ultimate Club.

This is why current nutrition data matters. Older Goodcents calorie values may not accurately represent a sandwich after a recipe or meat-portion change.

Bread Is a Major Part of the Sub

The bread contributes a substantial share of a Goodcents sandwich's carbohydrates and calories.

A sandwich calculation should therefore distinguish between:

Meat and cheese only

and

The complete sub with bread

Those are very different nutrition values.

If different bread choices are available at your location, use the option that actually matches your order whenever verified nutrition is available.

Standard Dressings and Condiments Add Up

Goodcents sandwiches can include ingredients such as lettuce, tomato, onion, mayonnaise, mustard, oil, and vinegar.

Vegetables generally make a relatively small calorie contribution, but mayo and oil can change the total much more noticeably.

That means:

Turkey + cheese + vegetables

can have a different nutrition profile from:

Turkey + cheese + vegetables + mayo + oil

even when the sub size stays the same.

When building a customized sandwich, account for the condiments you actually use.

Multi-meat subs often provide substantial protein, but sodium can also be high because several deli meats and cheese are combined in one sandwich.

A useful comparison therefore looks at:

A Meal Deal Is More Than a 4-Inch Sub

Goodcents currently offers a Meal Deal built around a 4-inch cold sub, a cookie or chips, and a 22 oz fountain drink .

That is important when calculating the full order.

The nutrition of the 4-inch sandwich alone does not represent the complete Meal Deal.

A useful calculation is:

4-inch sub + chips or cookie + beverage

The final calorie and sugar total can vary considerably depending on whether you select chips or a cookie and which drink you choose.

Box Lunches Need the Same Treatment

Current Goodcents catering menus also include Silver and Gold Box Lunches.

A Silver Box Lunch uses a 4-inch sub, while a Gold Box Lunch uses an 8-inch sub. Both can include additional items such as chips and a cookie.

If you are calculating one of these meals, count the components you personally consume rather than using only the sandwich nutrition.

Baked Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Baked Alfredo Pasta

These should have their own category in the nutrition calculator.

A bowl of chicken Alfredo has a completely different nutrition profile from a turkey or roast beef sub, so grouping pasta underneath a sandwich category makes the menu harder to understand.

Family Meals Need Serving Context

Goodcents also sells larger family meals and catering products.

These are designed for several people and should not be interpreted as single-person portions.

For example, a family pasta meal may include a large pasta quantity plus garlic bread and be intended to feed multiple people.

Where nutrition for the full package is available, NutritionSolver should clearly show:

Total package nutrition

and, where reasonably supported:

Approximate nutrition per stated serving

This prevents a multi-person meal from being mistaken for an individual entrée.

Chips and Cookies Can Change the Meal Quickly

Goodcents offers packaged chips and large fresh-baked cookies.

A cookie can add several hundred calories after the sandwich has already been counted. Chips add their own calories, carbs, fat, and sodium.

If you order a combo, box lunch, or Meal Deal, make sure the side is included in the calculation.

Drinks Need the Exact Beverage

A fountain drink should not have one universal calorie value.

Regular soda, diet soda, water, and other beverages have very different calories and sugar.

If the exact drink is known, use its own nutrition value.

If a generic menu item only says “fountain drink,” the calculator should show that calories vary by beverage instead of assigning zero calories to every possible choice.

After that, add your chips, cookie, pasta side, drink, or other meal components .
