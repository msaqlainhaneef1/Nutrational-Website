McAlister’s Deli meals often contain more than the item named on the menu.

A sandwich can include a side. A Giant Spud can come with butter and sour cream. A salad may need dressing counted separately. A Choose 2 combines portions from two different menu categories, while tea, cookies, chips, soup, or mac and cheese can add another part to the meal.

Use the McAlister’s Deli Nutrition Calculator above to compare individual items and build the complete order you actually eat.

Start With the Exact Sandwich

McAlister’s sandwiches use very different combinations of turkey, ham, roast beef, chicken salad, bacon, cheese, spreads, vegetables, and bread.

The McAlister’s Club, for example, combines turkey, Black Forest ham, bacon, cheddar, Swiss, vegetables, mayo, and McAlister’s Honey Mustard on wheat.

That is very different from a simpler turkey sandwich or roast beef sandwich.

When nutrition is available for the complete standard recipe, use that value first rather than trying to reconstruct the sandwich from generic deli ingredients.

Sandwich Calories May Include More Than the Sandwich

This is particularly important at McAlister’s.

McAlister’s Club Is a Major Menu Benchmark

The McAlister’s Club is one of the brand’s signature sandwiches.

The current official menu lists the standard McAlister’s Club at about 900 calories before further customizations are made.

Changing bread, removing mayo, replacing Honey Mustard, adding avocado, or adding another protein can alter the result.

For customized orders, calculate only the changes you actually make.

Spud Max Combines Several Toppings

The current Spud Max includes ham, turkey, bacon, cheddar-jack, green onions, and black olives, with butter and sour cream.

Its current standard nutrition is approximately 1,090 calories and 45g of protein .

Removing butter or sour cream changes the total. Adding avocado, chicken, ranch, cheese dip, or another topping changes it again.

This makes Giant Spuds particularly suitable for ingredient-level customization.

Choose 2 Needs Its Own Calculator Logic

The Choose 2 is one of the most important McAlister’s-specific features.

Instead of ordering one full entrée, you combine smaller portions from eligible menu categories.

A Choose 2 might pair:

Half sandwich + cup of soup

Half sandwich + salad

Spud portion + soup

or another supported combination.

Because the final nutrition depends on both selections, there is no useful universal calorie number for “McAlister’s Choose 2.”

The calculator should add the two exact portions selected.

Use Choose 2 Portions, Not Full Entrée Values

This serving distinction matters.

A sandwich can have:

Full sandwich nutrition

and a separate:

Choose 2 portion

The same can happen with starters or other eligible foods.

For example, a full entrée and its Choose 2 portion may differ dramatically.

NutritionSolver should clearly label these as separate serving sizes so users do not accidentally combine two full entrées while trying to calculate a Choose 2.

Starters Often Have Sharing Portions

Nachos and other starters can have serving definitions intended for multiple people.

For example, McAlister’s may distinguish between a Choose 2 portion and a larger starter designed to serve several people.

If a starter is labeled “Serves 2-4,” the full nutrition belongs to the whole platter.

If you share it, calculate the portion you personally eat rather than assigning yourself the entire dish.
