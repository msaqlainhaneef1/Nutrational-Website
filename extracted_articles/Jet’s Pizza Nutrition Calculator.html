Jet’s Pizza Nutrition Calculator

Jet’s Pizza nutrition depends heavily on how the pizza is built.

A Large Detroit-Style pizza, an 8 Corner Pizza®, a Hand-Tossed Round, Thin Crust, NY Style, or Cauliflower Crust all use different serving structures. Then sauce, cheese, toppings, specialty recipes, crust flavoring, Ranch, and the number of pieces you eat change the final total again.

Use the Jet’s Pizza Nutrition Calculator above to match your crust, pizza size, specialty recipe, and serving quantity, then add sides, wings, dips, drinks, or desserts for the complete order.

Detroit-Style Has Several Different Piece Sizes

Jet’s Detroit-Style pizza is the brand’s signature square pizza with a light, airy center and caramelized cheese around the crispy edges.

Current serving options include:

4 Corner Pizza®: 4 pieces

Large: 8 pieces

8 Corner Pizza®: 8 pieces

X-Large: 12 pieces

Party Tray: 24 pieces

The important detail is that these pieces are not all the same size.

Jet’s current nutrition menu lists the crust alone at approximately:

4 Corner Pizza® crust: 260 calories per piece

Large Detroit-Style crust: 210 calories per piece

8 Corner Pizza® crust: 260 calories per piece

X-Large crust: 210 calories per piece

Party Tray crust: 210 calories per piece.

These values are the starting point for a build-your-own pizza, not the complete nutrition of a finished cheese pizza.

Do Not Mistake Crust Calories for Cheese Pizza Calories

This is one of the most important details on a Jet’s nutrition page.

Jet’s official menu explicitly states that the base calories shown in its build-your-own section are “for crust only.” Cheese, sauce, and toppings add their own calories.

So a value such as:

Large Detroit-Style: 210 calories per piece

should not be interpreted as:

Finished Large Cheese Pizza: 210 calories per slice

unless the cheese and sauce are actually included in the source value.

For a custom pizza, calculate:

Crust + sauce + mozzarella + additional toppings

and then multiply by the number of pieces you eat.

Hand-Tossed Pizza Uses Different Slice Math

Jet’s Hand-Tossed Round pizza currently comes in:

Small 10-inch: 4 pieces

Medium 12-inch: 8 pieces

Large 14-inch: 8 pieces.

The official crust-only values are approximately:

Small: 210 calories per piece

Medium: 130 calories

Large: 180 calories.

A Small slice can therefore contain more crust than a Medium slice even though the pizza itself is smaller, because the pizzas are cut into a different number of pieces.

Always match both diameter and slice count .

Thin, NY Style, Cauliflower, and Gluten-Free Need Their Own Bases

Other Jet’s crusts use completely different serving structures.

Thin Crust Large: 120 calories

Seasoned Cauliflower Small: 90 calories

before the rest of the pizza is built.

Do not calculate one of these pizzas by subtracting calories from a Detroit-Style slice. Use the correct crust as the starting point.

Jet 10® Is a Much Different Pizza From Veggie

Jet 10® combines pepperoni, ham, hamburger, Italian sausage, bacon, mushrooms, onions, green peppers, and black olives with pizza sauce and mozzarella.

Veggie uses mushrooms, onions, green peppers, black olives, and tomatoes instead.

Both may use the same crust, but the finished calorie, protein, fat, and sodium values can be very different because the toppings are different.

Compare the actual specialty recipe instead of judging a pizza only by its crust.

Build Your Own Pizza Should Work Ingredient by Ingredient

The strongest Jet’s calculator structure is:

Crust style → size → sauce → cheese → toppings → pieces eaten

Jet’s officially lets customers choose crust, sauces, premium mozzarella, extra cheeses, and toppings when creating a custom pizza.

For example:

Large Detroit-Style + pizza sauce + mozzarella + pepperoni

should produce a different total from:

Large Detroit-Style + BBQ sauce + mozzarella + chicken + bacon

even though the base crust is identical.

Extra Cheese Has Its Own Calories

Premium mozzarella is standard on most pizzas, while feta, cheddar, and extra mozzarella can be added.

Jet’s current nutrition menu gives additional cheese calorie ranges because the amount used changes by crust and size.

If extra cheese is selected, it should be counted as an actual customization.

Do not simply use the standard cheese pizza value.

Jet’s Ranch Can Be a Major Addition

Jet’s Ranch is one of the brand’s most recognizable extras.

The current official menu lists a standard Ranch dipping serving at approximately 430 calories . Butter Garlic is about 190 calories , Blue Cheese about 280 , and Pizza Sauce about 100 calories .

That means:

3 pizza slices

and

3 pizza slices + full Ranch cup

are significantly different orders.

If Ranch is already part of a specialty pizza recipe, such as Chicken Bacon Ranch or Buffalo Ranch Chicken, do not add it twice. Add a separate serving only when you actually order extra Ranch.

Cajun Ranch Is a Current Menu Addition

Jet’s is currently promoting Cajun Ranch , which combines its house Ranch with Cajun seasoning.

Because it is a newer menu item, it should have its own nutrition record once a verified current value is available.

Do not automatically assign regular Ranch nutrition to Cajun Ranch unless Jet’s confirms the serving and formulation produce the same value.

Boneless Chicken Uses a Different Serving Definition

Boneless Chicken is not published using exactly the same serving unit as bone-in wings.

Jet’s current menu identifies a 10 oz serving , with nutrition shown on a piece-related basis in its menu materials.

A strong calculator should clearly state whether the displayed value represents:

one piece

one ounce

or

the entire serving

so users do not accidentally multiply the wrong number.

Calculate the Jet’s Pizza Order You Actually Eat

For pizza, start with the exact crust, size, specialty recipe or build-your-own ingredients, and number of pieces .

Then add any extra cheese, toppings, crust flavoring, Ranch or other dips .

Finally, include your wings, Jet’s Bread, Deli Boat, salad, drink, or dessert .

That produces a much more useful Jet’s nutrition total than relying on one generic calorie number for a pizza slice.
