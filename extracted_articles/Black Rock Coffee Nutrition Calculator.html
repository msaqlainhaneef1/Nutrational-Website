Black Rock Coffee Nutrition Calculator

Black Rock Coffee has everything from nearly calorie-free drip coffee to flavored lattes, blended Chillers, and Fuel Energy drinks. The nutrition difference between those choices can be substantial, especially once drink size, milk, flavors, and sweetness are taken into account.

Use the Black Rock Coffee Nutrition Calculator above to find your drink, compare sizes, and check calories, sugar, carbs, protein, and fat before you order.

The Drink Size Can Change the Numbers Quickly

At Black Rock Coffee, moving from one size to another does more than add a few extra ounces.

A larger flavored drink usually contains more milk, syrup, sauce, or other ingredients. That means its calories and sugar can rise noticeably.

For example, the nutrition database currently shows the Caramel Blondie increasing from about 420 calories for a 16 oz drink to 640 calories for a large .

When comparing drinks, always match the size in the calculator to the size you plan to order. A medium nutrition value should not be used as an estimate for a large drink.

Coffee Can Start Simple and Change With Customization

Plain brewed coffee, cold brew, and an Americano begin with very few calories.

A latte adds milk. A mocha adds chocolate ingredients. Signature drinks such as the Caramel Blondie, Blackout, Mexican Mocha, and Caramel Truffle can add flavored syrups, sauces, or richer dairy ingredients.

That is why two coffee drinks with the same espresso base can end up with very different nutrition totals.

Black Rock also allows guests to customize factors such as sweetness, milk type, espresso strength, flavors, and toppings. If your order differs from the standard recipe, the nutrition of the finished drink may differ too.

Fuel Energy Drinks Need Their Own Comparison

Black Rock's Fuel Energy drinks are different from its coffee menu and should be compared as their own category.

Fuel can be ordered iced or frozen, and flavored versions include options such as Blue Lagoon, Star Burst, Mai Tai, Sunrise, and other combinations.

Some Fuel drinks also include fruit flavors or juice, which can change the calorie and sugar content.

Black Rock offers regular and sugar-free Fuel options, but do not assume every flavored Fuel drink has the same nutrition simply because the base drink is similar. Check the specific version and preparation in the calculator.

Iced and Frozen Are Not Always Nutritionally Equal

A drink name may be available in more than one preparation.

An iced Fuel and a Frozen Fuel , for example, should not automatically be treated as the same nutrition entry. Blended drinks may use different ingredient quantities or preparation methods.

The same idea applies to coffee drinks that can be served hot, iced, or blended.

When several versions are listed, select the one that most closely matches your actual order rather than choosing by drink name alone.

Chillers and Smoothies Are More Than Flavored Coffee

Black Rock's blended menu includes products such as Chocolate Chip Chiller, Mint Chip Chiller, Oreo Chiller, smoothies, and shakes.

These drinks can have very different macro profiles from an Americano, cold brew, or standard latte.

For example, blended drinks may contain larger amounts of carbohydrate, sugar, or fat because of their bases and added ingredients.

If you are comparing two drinks with similar calories, check the sugar, carbs, fat, and protein as well. The calorie total alone does not show how the drinks differ.

Protein Drinks Have a Different Purpose

Black Rock now also offers a dedicated Protein Favorites range, including protein lattes, protein Caramel Blondie drinks, protein shakes, smoothies, and protein-boosted Fuel drinks.

These products should be treated separately from their standard versions because adding protein changes the drink's nutrition profile.

If protein is the main nutrient you are comparing, use the protein value for the exact drink rather than assuming that every milk-based coffee provides a similar amount.

Add Breakfast Food to the Full Order

Black Rock Coffee also serves all-day breakfast items at participating locations.

If your visit includes both a drink and a breakfast burrito, egg bites, muffin, bagel, or another food item, add both to the meal builder.

For example:

Latte + breakfast burrito

or

Cold brew + muffin

will produce a more realistic total than looking at the beverage alone.

Food availability can vary by location, so match the item with the menu at the store you are visiting.
