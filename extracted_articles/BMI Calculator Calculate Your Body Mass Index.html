What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index, or BMI, is a simple measurement that compares your weight with your height. It is commonly used as a screening tool to help identify whether an adult falls within an underweight, healthy weight, overweight, or obesity range.

Use the BMI Calculator above to calculate your BMI from your height and weight. Your result gives you a quick reference point, but it should not be treated as a complete measure of your health or body composition.

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated using your weight and height:

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in meters²

For example, if you weigh 70 kg and are 1.75 meters tall:

70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9

Your BMI would be approximately 22.9 .

The calculator performs this calculation automatically, so you only need to enter your height and weight to get your result.

What Does Your BMI Result Mean?

Your BMI can provide a useful starting point for understanding how your weight compares with your height.

However, BMI does not directly measure body fat. It also cannot distinguish between fat, muscle, bone, and other body tissues. This means two people with the same BMI may have very different body compositions.

People with a high amount of muscle, including some athletes, may have a higher BMI even when their body fat level is relatively low.

For a broader view of body composition, you may also want to use the Body Fat Calculator .

Is BMI Accurate?

BMI accurately calculates the relationship between height and weight, but it has limitations when used to assess an individual's health.

It does not consider factors such as muscle mass, body fat distribution, fitness level, age-related changes in body composition, or medical history.

For this reason, BMI is best used as a screening tool rather than a diagnosis .
