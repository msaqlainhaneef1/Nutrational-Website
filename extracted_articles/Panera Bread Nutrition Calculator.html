Panera Bread Nutrition Calculator

At Panera Bread, the serving label can be just as important as the menu item name.

A sandwich may come as a Half or Whole. A soup may be served as a Cup, Bowl, or inside a sourdough Bread Bowl. Salads and the newer Market Bowls can use Half and Whole portions. Then You Pick Two lets you combine smaller portions from different menu categories into one meal.

Use the Panera Bread Nutrition Calculator above to choose the exact serving you order and combine sandwiches, soups, salads, bowls, bagels, bakery items, breakfast foods, drinks, spreads, dressings, and sides.

Read the Serving Size Before the Calories

Panera publishes nutrition with specific serving descriptions such as:

1/2 Sandwich

1 Sandwich

1 Cup

1 Bowl

1 Bread Bowl with soup

1/2 Salad

1 Salad

1/2 Market Bowl

1 Market Bowl

These labels should never be hidden from the user.

For example, seeing:

Bacon Turkey Bravo: 430 calories

without saying Half Sandwich gives a completely different impression from the current Whole Sandwich at approximately 860 calories .

The serving format belongs directly beside the product name.

Half and Whole Sandwiches Should Be Separate

Many Panera sandwiches have distinct Half and Whole nutrition.

The current Bacon Turkey Bravo is approximately:

Half: 430 calories, 24g protein

Whole: 860 calories, 47g protein.

The current Ciabatta Cheesesteak is approximately:

Half: 580 calories

Whole: 1,160 calories.

The Chipotle Chicken Avo Melt is approximately:

Half: 500 calories

Whole: 1,000 calories.

A Half sandwich should therefore never inherit the Whole nutrition value or rely on a hidden serving multiplier.

Use the exact published serving.

You Pick Two Is a Meal Made From Smaller Portions

You Pick Two remains one of Panera's most important meal formats.

Panera currently describes it as a way to select two entrées from categories such as:

Soups

Salads

Sandwiches

and other eligible menu items.

The final nutrition depends on the two selections.

For example:

Half sandwich + Cup of soup

is very different from:

Half salad + another Half sandwich .

There is no useful universal calorie value for “Panera You Pick Two.”

NutritionSolver should calculate the two exact portions the customer selects.

Do Not Add Two Whole Entrées to a You Pick Two

This is a common serving error.

If you choose:

Bacon Turkey Bravo + Broccoli Cheddar

inside a You Pick Two order, you generally need the eligible smaller portions associated with that meal format rather than one Whole sandwich plus one full Bowl.

NutritionSolver should therefore distinguish:

Standard/full entrée

from:

You Pick Two portion

where Panera publishes different serving sizes.

This prevents the calculator from accidentally doubling the meal.

Mix & Match Is Not the Same Thing as You Pick Two

Panera introduced a separate Mix & Match value menu in 2026.

The current version uses selected Half sandwiches, Half salads, and Cups of soup, and guests can combine multiple eligible products. Panera has also confirmed that the broader You Pick Two remains available separately.

These are two different ordering concepts.

NutritionSolver should therefore use:

You Pick Two

and:

Mix & Match

as separate meal modes when needed rather than treating the names as interchangeable.

Soup Has Three Very Different Serving Formats

Soup is one of the clearest examples of why Panera needs serving-specific nutrition.

The current Broccoli Cheddar Soup is approximately:

Cup: 280 calories

Bowl: 420 calories

Bread Bowl: 930 calories.

The Bread Bowl total is much higher because the sourdough bread itself is part of the meal.

That is not simply a large soup.

It is:

Soup + edible sourdough bowl

and should have its own record.

The Bread Bowl Alone Has a Large Nutrition Contribution

Panera's current sourdough soup Bread Bowl is approximately:

650 calories

136g carbohydrates

21g protein

before the selected soup is considered in other calculation contexts.

This explains why changing a Cup of soup into a Bread Bowl can alter the meal dramatically.

NutritionSolver should never calculate a Bread Bowl by changing only the soup portion.

The bread itself must be included.

Broccoli Cheddar Cup and Bowl Are Not Interchangeable

Current Broccoli Cheddar nutrition is approximately:

Cup: 280 calories, 8g protein, 17g carbs, 20g fat, 1,010mg sodium

Bowl: 420 calories, 12g protein, 25g carbs, 31g fat, 1,520mg sodium.

That means both calories and sodium change meaningfully with serving size.

The item title should always state:

Cup

or:

Bowl .

Mac & Cheese Has the Same Portion Problem

Panera's current Mac & Cheese is approximately:

Cup: 490 calories

Do not use one generic “Panera Mac and Cheese calories” figure for all three.

Salads Need Half, Whole, Protein, and Dressing Context

Panera currently publishes Half and Whole nutrition for many salads.

Half: 270 calories

Half Caesar with Chicken: 340 calories

The protein changes the nutrition, and dressing is also an important component.

If dressing is included in the standard published salad, do not add it twice.

If the customer swaps or adds dressing, use the selected dressing portion.

Dressing Portions Are Designed Around Half and Whole Salads

Panera's current nutrition guide publishes different dressing quantities specifically for Half and Whole salads.

Location Matters at Panera
