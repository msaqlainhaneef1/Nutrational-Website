Biscuitville Nutrition Calculator

Biscuitville meals are built around more than the biscuit itself. Eggs, bacon, sausage, country ham, fried chicken, cheese, hashbrowns, grits, gravy, sauces, and drinks can all change the nutrition of your final breakfast.

Use the Biscuitville Nutrition Calculator above to compare menu items and build the nutrition for the breakfast you actually order.

Start With the Biscuit Filling

Two Biscuitville sandwiches can use the same scratch-made biscuit but still have very different calories and macros.

A plain biscuit on the current NutritionSolver menu has 260 calories , while fillings such as sausage, fried chicken, egg, cheese, or country ham add different amounts of protein, fat, and sodium.

That makes the filling just as important as the biscuit when comparing breakfast choices.

For example, the current database lists:

  • Bacon, Egg & Cheese Biscuit: 440 calories
  • Fried Chicken Biscuit: 470 calories
  • Sausage & Egg Biscuit: 480 calories
  • Fried Steak Biscuit: 580 calories

Compare the complete sandwich rather than assuming every biscuit-based item has a similar nutrition profile.

Breakfast Platters Need to Be Calculated as a Meal

Biscuitville also serves breakfast platters that can combine eggs, meat, a biscuit, and a side such as grits or hashbrowns. Its official menu currently includes both standard breakfast platters and larger BVL Ultimate Breakfast Platters.

Sodium Can Change Quickly Between Biscuit Choices

Calories do not tell the entire story at breakfast.

Several Biscuitville sandwiches in the current database contain more than 1,000mg of sodium. For example, the Country Ham Biscuit is listed at 1,270mg , while the Sausage Gravy Biscuit is listed at 1,480mg .

This is why it is worth comparing sodium separately when choosing between biscuits with similar calorie totals.

Meats, cheese, gravy, sauces, and seasoned sides can all contribute to the final sodium amount.

Bigger Biscuits Can Change More Than Portion Size

Biscuitville's BVL Ultimate line uses larger combinations, such as extra meat and cheese. The current official menu describes the Ultimate lineup as offering double meat and double cheese on selected biscuits.

Larger portions can increase protein, but they may also increase calories, fat, saturated fat, and sodium.

If you are deciding between a regular biscuit and an Ultimate version, compare the full nutrition profile rather than looking only at the extra protein.

Sides and Sauces Belong in the Calculation

Hashbrowns, grits, sausage gravy, country fries, cheese grits, ranch, honey mustard, and other extras are separate parts of the meal.

Biscuitville's current menu includes both classic and premium sides, along with multiple sauces and condiments.

If you add a side or dipping sauce, include it in the meal builder. Otherwise, the total shown for the main biscuit will not represent the complete breakfast.
