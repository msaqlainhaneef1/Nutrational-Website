Cold Press and Crafted Press Are Not the Same Drink

This is one of the most useful distinctions to understand on the Caribou menu.

Cold Press is Caribou's cold-brewed coffee and starts with very few calories when ordered without significant additions.

A Crafted Press starts with cold-pressed coffee but also includes milk or cream, a small amount of sugar and salt, creating a smoother and creamier drink.

That difference shows up clearly in the nutrition.

For example, the current NutritionSolver data lists a small iced Cold Press at around 15 calories , while a small hot Crafted Press contains about 100 calories .

So if you are comparing Caribou drinks, do not treat “Cold Press” and “Crafted Press” as two names for the same coffee.

Size Can Change More Than the Amount of Coffee

Caribou drinks are available in several sizes, and moving up a size can increase more than just volume.

More milk, flavoring, sweetener, cold foam, or other ingredients may be used in a larger drink.

The current calculator, for example, shows the hot Crafted Press increasing from about:

100 calories for a small

to

170 calories for a medium

and

200 calories for a large

The exact change depends on the drink, so always choose the size that matches your order rather than scaling a smaller drink by guesswork.

Mocha and Latte Calories Depend on What Is Added

A basic latte is built mainly from espresso and milk.

Caribou's flavored drinks can add chocolate, caramel, vanilla, whipped toppings, or other ingredients that substantially change the final nutrition.

This is especially important with drinks such as:

  • Turtle Mocha
  • Caramel High Rise
  • Campfire Mocha
  • Vanilla White Mocha
  • Caramel Espresso Shaker

Two espresso drinks may contain a similar amount of coffee but have very different calories, sugar, carbohydrates, and fat because of everything added around the espresso.

Compare the finished drink , not just the coffee base.

Caribou Energy Drinks Have a Different Nutrition Profile

Caribou's Energy drinks should be treated as a separate menu category rather than compared directly with coffee.

Current flavors include combinations such as fruit-flavored Energy drinks, lemonade Energy drinks, and versions topped with coconutmilk cold foam.

These drinks often get most of their calories from carbohydrates and sugar rather than fat or protein.

For example, the current calculator lists a medium Peach Mango Energy Drink at about 240 calories with 57g of carbohydrates and 56g of sugar .

Adding lemonade or cold foam can change those numbers further.

If you are choosing an Energy drink, pay particular attention to:

Drink size

Regular vs lemonade version

Cold foam

Total carbohydrates and sugar

Hot, Iced, and Blended Versions Need Separate Entries

Caribou offers several drinks in more than one preparation.

A drink may be available:

Hot

Iced

The nutrition is not always identical across all three.

Even when the drink name stays the same, differences in ice, milk, base ingredients, or preparation can change calories and macros.

Choose the exact preparation shown in the calculator rather than assuming the hot nutrition applies to the iced or blended version.

Milk and Cold Foam Can Change the Drink

Caribou beverages can also be customized with different milk options and additions such as oatmilk cold foam.

Changing the milk can alter:

  • Calories
  • Carbohydrates
  • Sugar
  • Fat
  • Protein

Cold foam adds another layer.

For example, NutritionSolver currently lists both standard Crafted Press drinks and Oatmilk Crafted Press with Oatmilk Cold Foam as separate entries because their nutrition profiles differ.

If your customized drink appears separately in the calculator, use that version rather than the standard recipe.

Blended Drinks Can Be Much More Calorie-Dense

Caribou Coolers and other frozen or blended beverages have a different nutrition profile from plain coffee or Cold Press.

The current database includes blended drinks that contain substantially more carbohydrates, sugar, and calories than the simpler coffee options.

This does not mean every blended beverage has the same nutrition. Flavor, size, toppings, milk, and preparation all matter.

The useful comparison is:

specific drink + specific preparation + specific size

rather than simply “coffee vs blended coffee.”

Add Breakfast or Lunch to See the Full Order

Caribou Coffee is not only a beverage menu.

Current food offerings include breakfast sandwiches, breakfast bowls, bakery items, and lunch choices. Caribou also promotes drink-and-food combinations that pair products such as Crafted Press with breakfast or Energy drinks with lunch.

If your visit includes food, calculate both parts.

For example:

Crafted Press + breakfast sandwich

Cold Press + donut

Energy drink + lunch item

can produce very different totals even when the beverage itself seems moderate.

Use the meal builder to combine the drink and food rather than treating your beverage as the entire order.
