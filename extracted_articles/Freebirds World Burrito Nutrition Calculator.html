Freebirds World Burrito Nutrition Calculator

Freebirds does not build every burrito the same way.

You choose the size, tortilla, protein, rice, beans, cheese, vegetables, queso, guacamole, and finishing sauces. That means two Freebird Burritos can have completely different calories and macros even though they share the same entrée name.

Use the Freebirds Nutrition Calculator above to build your order ingredient by ingredient and estimate the calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, sodium, fiber, and other available nutrition values in the meal you actually order.

Burrito Size Is the First Big Decision

Freebirds is known for offering burritos in several sizes rather than one standard portion.

Depending on the current menu and location, available sizes can include:

Halfbird

Freebird

Monster

Super Monster

Moving to a larger burrito can mean larger portions of the tortilla, protein, rice, beans, cheese, and other fillings.

For that reason, a Monster should not simply be calculated as a Freebird with a slightly larger tortilla.

The ingredient portions associated with the selected size need to be reflected throughout the calculation.

Do Not Treat “Freebird Burrito” as One Fixed Recipe

This is the most important rule for Freebirds nutrition.

A customer could build one Freebird Burrito with:

Flour tortilla + Spanish rice + black beans + Fajita White Chicken + cheese + pico

while another customer could order:

High Protein tortilla + cauliflower rice + steak + refried beans + queso + guacamole + Creamy Jalapeño

Both are technically Freebird Burritos, but their nutrition can differ substantially.

The calculator is most accurate when the entrée acts as a container for your selected ingredients rather than assigning every Freebird Burrito one universal calorie number.

The High Protein tortilla is especially distinctive because Freebirds markets it with 15g of protein .

NutritionSolver currently lists it at approximately 250 calories, 15g protein, and 30g carbohydrates .

By comparison, the current Flour Tortilla entry is approximately 270 calories with 7g protein and 44g carbohydrates .

So tortilla choice can change much more than flavor.

Black, Pinto, and Refried Beans Are Different

Freebirds offers black beans, whole pinto beans, and refried beans.

Current NutritionSolver values show:

Black Beans: about 95 calories and 6g protein

Pinto Beans: about 100 calories and 6g protein

Refried Beans: about 172 calories and 8g protein

Refried beans also have considerably more fat in the current data.

If you change the bean selection, update the calculator instead of treating all beans as nutritionally interchangeable.

Queso and Guacamole Deserve Separate Entries

Freebirds allows queso and guacamole to be added to customized entrées.

Current NutritionSolver entries list roughly:

Queso: 98 calories

Guacamole: 88 calories

These are not insignificant additions when combined with cheese, rice, beans, meat, and sauces.

If you order both queso and guacamole, include both.

The same applies to diced avocado if that is the option actually used in your meal.

A Bowl Removes the Tortilla, Not the Other Ingredients

Choosing a bowl instead of a burrito removes the large flour-style tortilla from the starting point.

But it does not automatically create a low-calorie order.

A bowl can still contain:

Rice + beans + steak + queso + cheese + guacamole + tortilla strips + creamy sauce

which can add up quickly.

Build the entire bowl before deciding how it compares with a burrito.

Freebirds Nachos Include Queso

Freebirds nachos follow a different structure from a bowl or salad.

The current menu states that nachos include two scoops of Liquid Gold Queso on the side , in addition to the selected protein and customizable toppings.

That serving definition matters.

If the nutrition entry already includes those two queso portions, do not add them again. If you are assembling the nachos from components, make sure the queso is included.

The Quesarito Needs Its Own Calculation

Freebirds also offers the Quesarito , which takes a burrito and fills it with melted queso before toasting it.

That makes it different from simply choosing a standard burrito and adding a small side of queso.

The Quesarito should ideally be available as its own build type:

Choose burrito size → add queso-filled preparation → choose remaining ingredients

This gives users a more accurate result than estimating it from a normal burrito.

High-Protein Burritos Use a Different Starting Recipe

Freebirds also currently promotes Protein-Packed Burritos using its High Protein tortilla and cauliflower rice.

Current combinations include steak, fajita chicken, and carnitas versions.

These meals have a distinct search intent because users may specifically be comparing protein rather than only calories.

Showing protein alongside calories is especially useful for these burritos.

Build Your Freebirds Order the Way You Actually Eat It

Start with your exact entrée and size , then choose the correct tortilla, rice, beans, protein, cheese, vegetables, queso, guacamole, and finishing sauces .

After that, add any chips and dip, dessert, kids item, or beverage that is part of the order.

That approach matches how Freebirds actually builds its food and gives you a much more useful nutrition total than relying on a single generic calorie number for a burrito.
