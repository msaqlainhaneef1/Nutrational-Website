Does Tropical Smoothie Cafe Add Sugar to Smoothies?

Tropical Smoothie Cafe allows customers to customize the sweetness of many made-to-order smoothies.

Depending on the order and location options, you may be able to request:

  • Regular sweetness
  • Half sugar
  • No added sugar
  • A sugar substitute

This can make a meaningful difference, especially with smoothies that already contain naturally occurring sugars from fruit.

There is an important distinction here.

No added sugar does not mean sugar-free.

Fruit naturally contains sugar. A smoothie made without added sweetener can therefore still contain a considerable amount of total sugar and carbohydrates.

If sugar or carbohydrates are important to you, look at the complete nutrition information rather than relying only on the phrase "no added sugar."

Fruit Sugar Still Counts in the Nutrition Total

Fruit provides vitamins, minerals, fiber, and other nutrients, but it also contributes carbohydrates and naturally occurring sugars.

When several fruits are blended into one large drink, the total carbohydrate amount can become significant.

That is why some fruit-based Tropical Smoothie Cafe smoothies contain much more sugar than people expect.

This does not make fruit something you need to avoid.

It simply means a smoothie should be counted as part of your overall meal rather than treated like flavored water.
