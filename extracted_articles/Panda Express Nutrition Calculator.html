Panda Express Nutrition Calculator

Panda Express nutrition makes the most sense when you calculate the meal by box configuration .

You do not normally build Orange Chicken one ingredient at a time. Instead, you choose a side, then add one, two, or three complete entrées depending on whether you order a Bowl, Plate, or Bigger Plate.

That means the most useful question is not simply, “How many calories are in Panda Express?”

It is:

Which side did I choose, which entrées did I add, and how many entrée portions are in my meal?

Use the Panda Express Nutrition Calculator above to combine the exact side, entrées, appetizers, sauces, drinks, and other items in your order.

First Choose the Meal Format

Panda Express currently uses a simple core meal structure:

Bowl: 1 side + 1 entrée

Plate: 1 side + 2 entrées

Bigger Plate: 1 side + 3 entrées

The current official menu lists broad calorie ranges of approximately:

Bowl: 280 to 1,130 calories

Plate: 430 to 1,640 calories

Bigger Plate: 580 to 2,150 calories

before considering some drink bundles or other extras.

Those ranges are wide because the side and entrée choices can be completely different.

A Super Greens Bowl with a lighter entrée is not nutritionally comparable with a Bigger Plate containing fried rice and three richer entrées.

The Side Can Be One of the Biggest Parts of the Meal

At Panda Express, the side is not a small garnish.

Current official nutrition lists approximately:

Chow Mein: 600 calories

Fried Rice: 620 calories.

That means the side alone can contribute more calories than many individual entrées.

For an accurate meal total, select the side first.

Chow Mein and Fried Rice Are Close in Calories but Not Identical

The current full-side serving of Chow Mein contains approximately:

600 calories

15g protein

94g carbohydrates

23g fat

1,000mg sodium .

The current Fried Rice serving contains approximately:

620 calories

13g protein

101g carbohydrates

19g fat

1,000mg sodium .

Their calories are similar, but the carbohydrate and fat profiles differ.

Choose the actual side rather than using one generic rice-or-noodle estimate.

Half-and-Half Needs Its Own Calculation

Many Panda Express customers split the side between two options.

For example:

Half Chow Mein + Half Super Greens

or:

Half Fried Rice + Half Chow Mein

A proper calculator should not treat that as a full portion of both sides.

The cleanest method is to calculate each side using the restaurant's serving proportion for a split side.

If Panda does not publish an exact half-side nutrition value for a specific combination, NutritionSolver should clearly label the result as calculated from the published full portions.

Orange Chicken Should Be Calculated as an Entrée Portion

The Original Orange Chicken remains Panda Express's signature entrée.

Current official nutrition lists one standard serving at approximately:

510 calories

16g protein

53g carbohydrates

24g fat

850mg sodium

20g sugar .

That is the entrée alone.

If you order Orange Chicken with Chow Mein, the side must be added separately.

Hot Orange Chicken Is a Separate Current Recipe

Panda Express currently also lists Hot Orange Chicken .

It is not simply regular Orange Chicken with zero-calorie spice added.

The current standard serving contains approximately:

550 calories

17g protein

59g carbohydrates

27g fat

950mg sodium .

NutritionSolver should therefore maintain separate records for:

Original Orange Chicken

and:

Hot Orange Chicken

while Hot Orange remains on the current menu.

A Bowl With Orange Chicken Is Side + Entrée

If you order:

Chow Mein + Orange Chicken

the meal calculation begins with:

600 calories from Chow Mein

plus:

510 calories from Orange Chicken

for approximately:

1,110 calories

before any appetizer, sauce, or beverage.

This example shows why a Bowl can still be a substantial meal even though it contains only one entrée.

A Plate Adds a Second Full Entrée

A Plate includes one side and two entrée selections.

For example:

Fried Rice + Orange Chicken + Broccoli Beef

should be calculated as three separate menu components.

Do not average Orange Chicken and Broccoli Beef.

Add both published entrée portions to the selected side.

Bigger Plate Is Not a Larger Scoop of One Entrée

The Bigger Plate adds a third entrée selection .

It should therefore work in the calculator as:

1 side + entrée 1 + entrée 2 + entrée 3

rather than a generic multiplier applied to a normal Plate.

This also lets a customer correctly calculate three different entrées.

Broccoli Beef Has a Much Different Profile From Orange Chicken

Current Broccoli Beef contains approximately:

150 calories

15g protein

12g carbohydrates

6g fat

520mg sodium per standard serving.

Compare that with the current 510-calorie Orange Chicken.

Both count as one entrée choice in a standard meal, but they contribute very different nutrition.

This is why entrée count alone does not tell you the meal calories.

Honey Walnut Shrimp Is a Premium Entrée With Its Own Serving

Current Honey Walnut Shrimp contains approximately:

430 calories

13g protein

32g carbohydrates

28g fat

700mg sodium .

Premium entrée status is a pricing/menu distinction, not a nutrition category.

Use the actual nutrition for the shrimp rather than assuming premium entrées are automatically larger or smaller than standard entrées.

Grilled Teriyaki Chicken Is Protein-Dense

Current official nutrition lists Grilled Teriyaki Chicken at approximately:

275 calories

33g protein

14g carbohydrates

10g fat

470mg sodium .

That gives it a different calorie-to-protein relationship from Orange Chicken or Honey Walnut Shrimp.

However, teriyaki sauce can also be added separately, so make sure the selected nutrition record matches the way the restaurant publishes the chicken.

Extra Teriyaki Sauce Should Not Be Forgotten

Panda Express currently lists a separate Teriyaki Sauce serving at approximately:

70 calories

16g carbohydrates

14g sugar

380mg sodium .

If your chicken nutrition does not include the extra sauce packet you use, add it separately.

One sauce packet can noticeably change sugar and sodium even when it does not look like a large part of the meal.

Kung Pao Chicken Has Moderate Calories but High Sodium

Current Kung Pao Chicken contains approximately:

320 calories

17g protein

15g carbohydrates

21g fat

1,050mg sodium .

That is a useful reminder that a lower calorie entrée is not automatically lower in sodium.

When building a multi-entrée Plate, sodium can increase quickly across several wok-fired dishes.

Super Greens Should Be a First-Class Side

Super Greens is currently one of Panda Express's key vegetable options.

The official nutrition table lists the standard entrée-sized portion at approximately:

90 calories

6g protein

10g carbohydrates

5g fiber

260mg sodium .

It should appear prominently in NutritionSolver's side options rather than being replaced by older generic Mixed Vegetables records.

Appetizers Need Piece Count

Panda Express appetizers use defined serving quantities.

Current examples include approximately:

Chicken Egg Roll, 1 roll: 200 calories

Chicken Potstickers, 3 pieces: 160 calories

Cream Cheese Rangoon, 3 pieces: 190 calories

Vegetable Spring Rolls, 2 rolls: 240 calories.

The piece count belongs directly in the item name.

A user eating one spring roll should not automatically receive the nutrition of the full two-piece published serving.

Sauces Are Small but Sodium Can Be High

Current Panda Express sauce examples include:

Soy Sauce: about 5 calories and 375mg sodium

Hot Mustard: about 10 calories and 115mg sodium

Potsticker Sauce: about 10 calories and 290mg sodium

Sweet & Sour Sauce: about 70 calories and 20g sugar

Teriyaki Sauce: about 70 calories and 380mg sodium.

This is another place where calories alone can be misleading.

A sauce can contribute almost no calories yet add several hundred milligrams of sodium.

Cub Meals Use Junior Portions

Panda Cub Meals are not simply smaller adult Plates.

Panda currently defines a Cub Meal as:

1 junior side

1 junior entrée

fruit side

bottled water or kids juice .

The official nutrition database publishes specific junior portions for many sides and entrées.

For example, current Orange Chicken in a Cub Meal uses a smaller portion than the standard adult entrée.

NutritionSolver should use those junior records instead of mathematically dividing adult portions.

Complete Cub Meals Can Already Have Published Nutrition

Panda currently publishes complete standard Cub Meal examples.

For instance:

Broccoli Beef Cub Meal: approximately 300 calories

Grilled Teriyaki Chicken Cub Meal: approximately 400 calories

Orange Chicken Cub Meal: approximately 580 calories

with the stated standard side, fruit, and drink configuration.

Where an official complete meal value exists, use it rather than rebuilding the same standard combination unnecessarily.

Balanced Protein Plates Are a Different Current Menu Format

Panda Express currently offers Balanced Protein Plates , described as pre-set plates designed around specific combinations.

Current official ordering pages show a broad range of approximately:

460 to 875 calories per serving .

These should be maintained as their own current meal category rather than being hidden among ordinary à la carte entrées.

If a customer changes the pre-set plate, use a custom Plate calculation.

Family Meals Need Shared-Meal Logic

The current 5 Person Family Meal contains:

2 large sides

3 large entrées .

Those are family-sized components, not normal individual entrée portions.

NutritionSolver should distinguish:

whole Family Meal nutrition

from:

estimated per-person portion

when dividing the meal among diners.

Never show a family package total as though it represents one standard Plate.

A La Carte Portions Need Their Own Size Labels

Panda also sells sides and entrées à la carte.

The current ordering site gives an a la carte range of approximately:

130 to 620 calories per serving depending on the item.

If Small, Medium, and Large à la carte sizes are available for a particular product, keep those serving sizes separate.

Do not reuse the standard Plate scoop for a large catering-style entrée.

Drinks Can Change a Bundle Dramatically

Panda Bundles can add a medium fountain drink to a Bowl, Plate, or Bigger Plate.

Current fountain drink nutrition varies widely.

For example, the official database lists:

Diet Coke, Medium: approximately 0 calories

Dr Pepper, Medium: approximately 360 calories

Sweet Tea, Medium: approximately 410 calories.

A Panda Bundle therefore needs the exact beverage selection to produce a useful total.

Beverage Size Matters Too

The current official nutrition table distinguishes Kids, Small, Medium, and Large fountain servings.

A Large sweetened beverage can contain several hundred more calories than a smaller serving.

Select both:

beverage + size

rather than using a generic Soft Drink calorie value.

Calculate the Panda Box You Actually Ordered

Start by selecting the exact Bowl, Plate, Bigger Plate, Cub Meal, Balanced Protein Plate, or Family Meal format .

Then choose the correct side .

Add each entrée portion individually , including premium or limited-time entrées where applicable.

Finally, include the appetizers, sauce packets, fountain drink, or other extras you actually consume.

Panda Express is especially well suited to this component-based approach because the same Orange Chicken entrée can become a completely different meal depending on the side and how many additional entrées share the box.
