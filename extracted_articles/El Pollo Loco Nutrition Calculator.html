El Pollo Loco Nutrition Calculator

El Pollo Loco nutrition starts with its fire-grilled chicken, but the chicken is often only one part of the meal.

A chicken breast with broccoli has a very different nutrition profile from a two-piece chicken meal with rice, beans, flour tortillas, and salsa. Bowls, burritos, quesadillas, nachos, and family meals combine the same core ingredients in different portions.

Use the El Pollo Loco Nutrition Calculator above to compare individual foods or combine the chicken, sides, tortillas, salsa, drinks, and other items that make up your actual order.

Fire-Grilled Chicken Pieces Are Not Nutritionally Identical

El Pollo Loco sells individual breasts, thighs, legs, and wings, and each cut has its own calorie and macro profile.

Current official nutrition values list approximately:

Chicken Wing: 70 calories and 10g protein
Chicken Leg: 110 calories and 11g protein
Chicken Thigh: 200 calories and 21g protein
Chicken Breast: 200 calories and 34g protein

The breast provides considerably more protein than the smaller pieces, while the thigh has a different fat profile.

If your meal contains several pieces, calculate the actual combination rather than multiplying one generic “chicken piece” value.

Chicken Meals Need the Sides and Tortillas

A two-piece chicken meal is more than the chicken itself.

Depending on your order, you may also receive rice, beans, tortillas, and other sides. Changing from corn to flour tortillas can alter the meal even when everything else stays the same.

Your current NutritionSolver data shows this clearly:

Breast & Wing Meal with corn tortillas, pinto beans and rice: 660 calories

The same meal with flour tortillas: 740 calories

The protein is almost unchanged, but calories, carbohydrates, fat, and sodium increase because the tortilla changes.

This makes tortilla choice worth tracking instead of treating it as a negligible extra.

Corn and Flour Tortillas Have Different Nutrition

The current official guide lists two corn tortillas at about 90 calories , compared with about 170 calories for two flour tortillas .

Flour tortillas also contribute substantially more sodium in the standard serving.

If tortillas are already included inside a complete meal nutrition entry, do not add them again. If you are assembling the meal from separate chicken and side components, include the tortillas you actually eat.

Bowls Can Be High in Protein and High in Carbs at the Same Time

El Pollo Loco bowls combine chicken with ingredients such as rice, beans, cheese, avocado, queso, corn, or other toppings.

Current official examples include:

Original Pollo Bowl: 580 calories, 41g protein, 83g carbs

Double Chicken Bowl: 930 calories, 74g protein

Grande Avocado Chicken Bowl: 790 calories, 49g protein

Queso Crunch Double Chicken Burrito Bowl: 1,080 calories, 78g protein

A high-protein bowl is therefore not automatically a low-calorie or low-carbohydrate bowl.

Compare protein together with the entire calorie and macro profile.

Burritos Add Tortilla, Sauce, Cheese, and Avocado

El Pollo Loco's current burrito lineup includes options such as the Guacamole Chicken Burrito, Chipotle Guacamole Chicken Burrito, Queso Guacamole Chicken Burrito, BRC Burrito, and Classic Chicken Burrito.

Current official nutrition lists approximately:

Guacamole Chicken Burrito: 920 calories and 59g protein

Chipotle Guacamole Chicken Burrito: 1,010 calories and 60g protein

Queso Guacamole Chicken Burrito: 780 calories and 40g protein

The differences come from the full recipe, not simply the chicken.

Use the exact burrito instead of applying one average calorie figure to every El Pollo Loco burrito.

Quesadillas and Nachos Can Be Larger Than a Chicken Meal

Items that sound like sides or snacks can contain substantial calories.

The current official nutrition guide lists:

Cheese Quesadilla: 530 calories

Salsa Verde Chicken Quesadilla: 810 calories

Chipotle Chicken Quesadilla: 930 calories

Shredded Chicken Nachos: 1,090 calories

This is another reason the menu category alone does not tell you how large the nutrition total will be.

Compare the exact serving and ingredients.

Salsa Adds Fewer Calories, but Sodium Still Matters

El Pollo Loco's salsas are relatively low in calories.

Current servings include approximately:

Salsa Fresca: 5 calories

Salsa Roja: 15 calories

Avocado Salsa: 20 calories

But low calorie does not mean nutritionally zero. Salsa can still contribute sodium, and using several portions increases the amount.

If you use multiple containers, add the number of servings you actually consume.

Salad Calories May Not Include Dressing

This is an important El Pollo Loco serving detail.

The current nutrition guide marks several salads with an asterisk indicating that dressing is not included in the listed entrée nutrition.

For example, the Mexican Caesar Salad is currently listed at about 420 calories and 52g protein before dressing .

Adding Creamy Cilantro, House Ranch, Mexican Caesar, or another dressing changes the final calories, fat, and sodium.

Always check whether dressing is already included before calculating your salad.

Family Meals Should Be Calculated by What You Personally Eat

El Pollo Loco currently offers 8-piece, 12-piece, and 16-piece family meals.

The 8-piece meal is designed for approximately 3 to 4 people and includes two large sides. The 12-piece meal is positioned for 5 to 6 people with three large sides, while the 16-piece meal is intended for roughly 7 to 8 people and also includes three large sides, plus tortillas and salsa.

A family meal should therefore not be treated as one personal serving.

A better approach is to calculate:

Chicken pieces you ate + your share of sides + tortillas + salsa

rather than assigning yourself the nutrition for the entire family package.

Side Choice Can Reshape the Plate

El Pollo Loco currently offers sides with very different calorie profiles.

Examples from the 2026 nutrition guide include approximately:

Broccoli: 30 calories

Mashed Potatoes with Gravy: 100 calories

Coleslaw: 130 calories

Pinto Beans: 140 calories

Corn: 140 calories

Rice: 160 calories

Macaroni & Cheese: 310 calories

That creates a large difference between two otherwise identical chicken meals.

When comparing meal combinations, choose the exact side rather than applying one generic side value.

Pay Attention to “Entrée Only”

El Pollo Loco's official nutrition guide specifically labels some Fire-Grilled Combos as entrée only .

For example, the Original Pollo Bowl, BRC Burrito, Tacos al Carbón, and Classic Chicken Burrito can appear in combo contexts, but their listed nutrition does not necessarily represent every side or drink sold with the promotion.

The same principle applies to certain Kids Meals.

Always check the serving definition before assuming a combo calorie number represents the entire order.
