la Madeleine Nutrition Calculator

At la Madeleine, the name of a dish does not always tell you how much food is included in the published nutrition.

A breakfast can include eggs, croissant, meat, and potatoes. A sandwich may be available as a half or whole serving. Soups come in cups and bowls. A Café Sampler combines several smaller items, while a pastry or coffee drink can add a completely separate nutrition total.

Use the la Madeleine Nutrition Calculator above to match the serving you actually order and compare calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, sodium, and other available nutrients.

Start by Checking What the Meal Includes

Serving definitions matter especially at la Madeleine.

The Country French Breakfast, for example, combines eggs, a Potato Galette, breakfast meat, and a butter croissant rather than representing eggs alone. The current menu describes the American Breakfast similarly, with eggs, bacon or sausage, and a butter croissant.

Before adding sides separately, check whether they are already included in the nutrition entry.

Otherwise, it is easy to count the same croissant, fruit, potatoes, or breakfast meat twice.

Coffee Customizations Belong in the Drink Total

La Madeleine also has a substantial coffee and espresso menu, and your current NutritionSolver page contains separate coffee customization records.

Plain brewed coffee may contribute very few calories, but milk, cream, syrups, chocolate, whipped toppings, and other additions can substantially change the finished beverage.

A useful drink calculator should follow:

Drink + size + milk + flavor + other additions

rather than providing one generic coffee calorie number.
