Calories are useful, but they are only part of a restaurant meal's nutrition profile.

Individual restaurant pages let you compare menu items using protein, carbs, fat, sodium, fiber, sugar, saturated fat, and cholesterol where those values are available.

This makes it easier to answer practical questions such as which menu item has more protein, how much sodium a meal contains, or how two similar choices compare nutritionally.

Why Restaurant Nutrition Numbers Can Vary

Restaurant nutrition information should be treated as a useful estimate.

Actual portions can vary slightly by location and preparation. Customizations, toppings, sauces, drink sizes, substitutions, and limited-time recipes can also change the final nutrition of an order.

When a restaurant changes its recipe or publishes updated nutrition information, the numbers may also differ from older menus or third-party listings.

For the most useful comparison, make sure the menu item, size, and customization shown on the page match what you plan to order.

Where the Restaurant Nutrition Data Comes From

NutritionSolver compiles restaurant nutrition information from public nutrition disclosures published by restaurant chains and organizes the data into searchable menu pages.

The goal is simple: make restaurant calories and nutrition facts easier to find, compare, and combine without searching through multiple menus or nutrition documents.
