Build the Meal You Actually Order

The easiest way to understand ALBAIK calories is to calculate the order piece by piece.

Start with your main item, such as a chicken meal, nuggets, sandwich or seafood option. Then add any fries, hummus, coleslaw, sauces, desserts or drinks you plan to have.

NutritionSolver will combine the selected items so you can see the total calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat for the meal instead of estimating each item separately.

This is particularly useful when comparing a full meal with a sandwich or smaller order, because portion sizes can be very different.

Chicken Meals, Nuggets and Sandwiches Are Not Equivalent

ALBAIK offers several ways to order chicken, including multi-piece chicken meals, fillet nuggets, burgers, sandwiches and other chicken-based menu items.

Even when two options contain chicken, their nutrition can differ considerably because of:

  • Portion size
  • Breading and preparation
  • Bread or buns
  • Included sides
  • Cheese or other toppings
  • Sauces

For that reason, it is better to compare the specific menu items above rather than assuming every chicken option has a similar calorie or protein count.

Do Not Forget the Sauces and Sides

A common mistake when calculating restaurant nutrition is counting the main item but leaving out the extras.

At ALBAIK, additions such as garlic sauce, cocktail sauce, nugget sauce and fries contribute their own calories and macros to the final order.

If you use more than one sauce or order several sides, add each one to the meal builder. This gives you a much more realistic total than looking only at the chicken or sandwich.

ALBAIK Also Has Seafood and Meat-Free Options

The ALBAIK menu is not limited to fried chicken.

Depending on location and availability, the menu can include fish fillet items, shrimp meals and sandwiches, falafel, hummus, corn and other sides .

These foods have different combinations of protein, carbohydrates and fat, so use the category filters to compare options within the type of food you are actually considering.

Why ALBAIK Nutrition Can Differ by Location

ALBAIK operates across multiple markets, and menu availability can differ between countries and restaurants.

Recipes, portion sizes, limited-time products and available menu items can also change. A nutrition value found for one product or market should not automatically be assumed to apply to every ALBAIK location.

When possible, match the item name and portion shown in the calculator with the product available at your restaurant.
