Port of Subs Nutrition Calculator

At Port of Subs, ordering the right sandwich number is only the beginning of the nutrition calculation.

The same Classic Sub can come as a 5-inch Small, 8-inch Medium, 12-inch Large, 24-inch X-Large, wheat wrap, lettuce wrap, or salad. Bread choice and toppings can change the finished order again.

That makes sub number + serving format + size the most useful way to calculate a Port of Subs meal.

Use the Port of Subs Nutrition Calculator above to select the exact sandwich, size, bread or wrap format, toppings, side, dessert, and drink you order.

Start With the Sub Number

Port of Subs currently organizes its core cold sandwiches into 18 numbered Classic Subs .

Examples include:

#1: Ham, Salami, Capicolla, Pepperoni and Provolone

#2: Ham, Turkey and Provolone

#6: Three cheeses and avocado

#8: Turkey and Provolone

#9: Peppered Pastrami and Swiss

#10: Roasted Chicken Breast and Provolone

#17: Tuna Salad with your choice of cheese

#18: Roast Beef, Turkey and Provolone

Do not calculate all turkey, ham, salami, or roast beef subs from one generic deli-sandwich value.

Choose the exact numbered recipe first.

Size Changes More Than the Bread Length

Port of Subs publishes separate nutrition for:

5" Small

8" Medium

12" Large

24" X-Large

The meat, cheese, bread and default toppings are portioned for the selected sandwich size.

That means you should use the actual published serving rather than multiplying a 5-inch sandwich by a simple mathematical factor.

Bread Choice Should Be a Customization

Current Port of Subs menu options include breads such as:

White

Wheat

Sourdough

with additional breads available in some locations or data sets.

Bread contributes a large share of a sub's carbohydrates and calories.

For example, the same filling on a different bread should not automatically retain the exact same nutrition.

A strong calculator should let you:

Choose sub → choose size → choose bread → customize toppings

rather than create hundreds of disconnected sandwich records.

Wheat Does Not Automatically Make the Whole Sandwich Low Calorie

Switching from white to wheat bread changes the bread's nutrition profile, but the finished sub still contains its meat, cheese, oil, dressing, and other ingredients.

The bread choice is only one part of the meal.

Compare the entire finished sandwich rather than judging it from the bread name alone.

Lettuce Wraps Change the Format Dramatically

Port of Subs lets customers order Classic Subs as lettuce wraps.

The brand currently promotes this option as saving about 300 calories compared with the standard sandwich format.

That makes the serving format a major calculator decision.

A Classic Sub #8 on bread and Classic Sub #8 in lettuce are still based on the same sandwich concept, but their carbohydrate and calorie totals differ substantially.

Classic Sub #8 Is a Good Example

The standard turkey and Provolone Classic Sub #8 can be ordered as a regular sub or lettuce wrap.

The current official nutrition data lists the 5-inch Small standard #8 at approximately:

360 calories

26g protein

260 calories

The sandwich identity remains the same, but removing the bread changes the carbohydrate profile considerably.

Wheat Wraps Are Another Distinct Format

Port of Subs also allows Classic Subs to be ordered as wheat wraps.

A wheat wrap should not use the calorie value of the 5-inch, 8-inch, or 12-inch bread-based sandwich.

The official nutrition system publishes wrap nutrition separately.

The calculator should therefore ask:

Sub

then:

Bread Sub | Wheat Wrap | Lettuce Wrap | Salad

before calculating the final order.

A Salad Can Be Based on Your Favorite Classic Sub

Port of Subs currently lets customers turn a Classic Sub into a salad.

That gives the restaurant an unusual nutrition structure.

A #2 salad still carries the ham, turkey and cheese identity of Classic Sub #2, but without the standard bread.

This should be modeled as a format conversion , not as an unrelated salad.

A useful flow is:

Choose Classic Sub → Convert to Salad → Choose Dressing

Dressing Needs Its Own Calculation

Port of Subs offers Garden, Spinach, Caesar, Chef, Tuna and chicken-based salads.

Some salads come with a specified dressing, while others let the customer choose.

The dressing can contribute a meaningful amount of calories, fat, sodium or sugar.

If the published salad nutrition excludes dressing, add the selected dressing separately.

If the standard nutrition already includes it, do not add the same serving twice.

Chicken Melt Flavors Change More Than the Name

Port of Subs currently offers several versions of its grilled chicken melt.

The standard Chicken Melt uses grilled chicken and Provolone.

Teriyaki adds Sweet Teriyaki sauce.

Buffalo adds Buffalo sauce.

BBQ adds BBQ sauce.

The added sauce can change carbohydrates, sugar and sodium even when the chicken and cheese remain similar.

Use the exact named melt.

Buffalo Chicken Can Be Especially High in Sodium

The current official nutrition data shows that sauce selection can materially affect sodium.

For example, the 8-inch Grilled Chicken Melt is published at roughly 1,700mg sodium , while the 8-inch Buffalo version is around 3,950mg .

That is a much larger difference than the calorie totals alone suggest.

For Port of Subs, sodium deserves to remain visible beside calories and macros.

Chicken Cordon Bleu Is a Current Limited-Time Product

Some Port of Subs locations currently feature a Chicken Cordon Bleu Sub made with grilled chicken, ham, Swiss cheese and honey mustard.

Because it is promotional rather than one of the permanent numbered Classic Subs, it should be stored under:

Current Limited-Time

with an availability or review date.

When the promotion ends, it should move to a historical section rather than remain permanently mixed into the core menu.

Turkey Bacon Avocado Has Its Own Recipe

The current specialty menu includes Turkey, Bacon & Avocado with Provolone .

This should not be estimated using Classic Sub #8 simply because both contain turkey and Provolone.

Bacon and avocado create a different finished recipe.

Keep it as a separate specialty sandwich.

Turkey Bacon Ranch Wrap Is Not a Converted Classic Sub

The current Turkey Bacon Ranch Wrap contains turkey, bacon and ranch dressing inside a wheat tortilla.

It is a named specialty wrap, not simply one of the numbered Classic Subs converted to wheat.

Use its own published nutrition.

Grilled Chicken Caesar Wrap Is Also a Complete Recipe

Grilled chicken

Lettuce

Parmesan

The dressing and Parmesan are already part of the standard recipe.

Do not rebuild it as plain grilled chicken plus a generic tortilla unless the customer modifies the sandwich.

Breakfast Needs Bread and Protein Choice

Port of Subs currently lists breakfast favorites such as:

Peppered Bacon, Egg & Cheese

Smoked Ham, Egg & Cheese

Sausage, Egg & Cheese .

The choice of bacon, ham or sausage changes the nutrition, and the bread or wrap format can change it again.

Breakfast should therefore use:

Breakfast recipe → size or wrap → cheese/bread customization

rather than one generic breakfast-sub value.

Sausage, Egg & Cheese Can Become a Large Meal

The nutrition database includes approximately:

5" Sausage, Egg & Cheese: 690 calories

8" Sausage, Egg & Cheese: 1,090 calories.

That illustrates why breakfast sandwich size needs to be displayed prominently.

An 8-inch breakfast sub should not be mistaken for a small breakfast sandwich.

Build the Port of Subs Order the Way It Is Sliced

Then choose the serving format:

5" Small

8" Medium

12" Large

24" X-Large

Wheat Wrap

or:

After that, account for the actual bread, toppings, dressing and customization .

Finally, add the chips, macaroni salad, potato salad, dessert and drink that are part of your meal.

Port of Subs has detailed nutrition because one numbered sandwich can exist in many serving formats. Keeping those formats connected under the same sandwich is the clearest way to turn that data into a useful calculator.
