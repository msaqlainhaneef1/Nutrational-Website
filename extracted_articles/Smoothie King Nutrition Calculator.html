Smoothie King Nutrition Calculator

Smoothie King does not organize its menu around flavor alone.

Its smoothies are built around different goals such as Get Fit, Feel Energized, Manage Weight, Be Well, Fruit Classics, GLP-1, and Kids . Then the nutrition can change again when you choose a larger cup, remove an ingredient, swap part of the recipe, or add an enhancer.

Use the Smoothie King Nutrition Calculator above to choose the exact smoothie, serving size, and additions you order.

For the clearest comparison, check not only calories and protein but also total carbohydrates, fiber, total sugar, added sugar, sodium, and caffeine .

Choose the Smoothie Before You Choose the Size

Smoothie King's current nutrition system calculates each recipe separately.

For example, the current 20 oz Gladiator Vanilla contains approximately:

220 calories

45g protein

3g carbohydrates

2.5g fat

1g sugar

0g added sugar

400mg sodium .

The Chocolate version is also about 220 calories and 45g protein, but its fat, carbohydrates, sodium, and caffeine differ.

A smoothie name therefore needs its own nutrition profile even when two products have the same calories.

20 oz Is Only One Serving Size

Current Smoothie King product pages commonly offer:

20 oz

32 oz

and:

44 oz

smoothies.

Some promotional or partnership items can use different size structures.

The nutrition figures shown on the main menu are generally based on the 20 fl oz serving , while Smoothie King's online calculator recalculates the label when another cup size is selected.

That serving size should always be visible next to the nutrition number.

A user searching for a 32 oz smoothie should not accidentally receive a 20 oz calorie total.

Do Not Simply Double the 20 oz Number

A larger smoothie should use Smoothie King's published size-specific calculation.

Do not assume:

40 oz = exactly 2 × 20 oz

or that a 32 oz serving is exactly 1.6 times every nutrient.

Recipe scaling, ice, ingredient scoops, enhancers, and preparation can prevent every nutrient from scaling perfectly.

Use the official size-specific result whenever it is available.

Build the Smoothie Around Your Actual Order

For smoothies:

Choose Purpose → Recipe → Cup Size → Remove/Swap Ingredients → Add Enhancers

For bowls:

Choose Bowl → Verify Standard Toppings → Add or Remove Extras

For Power Eats:

Choose Food → Recipe or Sauce → Dipping Sauce where applicable

Then combine the smoothie, bowl, or food items that are actually part of your order.

Smoothie King publishes detailed live nutrition and updates the label as recipes are customized. The most useful NutritionSolver experience is therefore not one static number per smoothie. It is a size-aware, customization-aware calculator that preserves the distinction between total sugar, added sugar, protein, caffeine, and the exact recipe being ordered.
