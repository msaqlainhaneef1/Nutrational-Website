Red Lobster Nutrition Calculator

Red Lobster nutrition is easiest to understand when you know what the published calorie number already includes and what still needs to be added .

Many current entrées include fixed sauces, condiments, or dipping sauces in the published nutrition, but the restaurant lists your chosen side dishes separately. Other meals, such as Create Your Own Ultimate Feast, Seafood Boils, Crab Your Way, and family feasts, are designed from several selectable components.

Use the Red Lobster Nutrition Calculator above to match the exact seafood, preparation, sides, sauces, biscuits, and drinks you actually consume.

Do Not Assume the Entrée Number Is the Entire Dinner

Red Lobster currently states that nutritional content for its entrées includes fixed components such as condiments and dipping sauces, while customer-selected side choices are listed separately .

This distinction matters.

If an entrée is listed at 510 calories and you choose two sides, you should not assume the complete plate is still 510 calories.

A useful Red Lobster calculation usually looks like:

Entrée nutrition

plus:

Side 1

plus:

Side 2

plus any:

extra seafood, additional sauce, biscuits, beverage, appetizer, or dessert

that you consume.

Build the Red Lobster Dinner in the Correct Order

For a standard entrée:

Choose entrée → Side 1 → Side 2 → Extra seafood → Biscuits → Drink/Dessert

For Create Your Own Ultimate Feast:

Premium 1 → Premium 2 → Classic 1 → Classic 2 → Sides

For a Seafood Boil:

Boil → Crab type → Flavor → Spice level → Add-ons → Extra dunking sauce

For Endless Shrimp:

Count every shrimp selection and refill actually eaten

For family meals:

Build the group order first, then calculate the portion personally eaten

Red Lobster has unusually detailed current nutrition information. The key is not simply collecting more numbers. It is preserving what each number represents so the diner does not confuse an entrée base, a selected side, a boil flavor, a family portion, or a complete meal.
