Nando’s Nutrition Calculator

Nando’s nutrition starts with a decision that is different from most chicken restaurants: which cut of flame-grilled chicken do you want?

A quarter breast, quarter leg, half chicken, boneless breast, thigh skewer, or wings can all provide very different calories and protein before you add PERi Chips, Portuguese Rice, Garlic Bread, PERi Mac, sauce, or another side.

Then comes the heat level.

Nando’s marinates its chicken in PERi-PERi for 24 hours and flame-grills it to order with the heat level you choose. That makes the chicken cut, portion, baste, and sides more useful calculation inputs than one generic “Nando’s chicken calories” number.

Use the Nando’s Nutrition Calculator above to match the exact chicken, sandwich, bowl, side, sauce, and serving size you actually order.

Heat Is a Flavor Choice, but Baste Still Matters

Nando’s lets customers choose their preferred PERi-PERi heat.

Depending on the current menu and location, choices can include milder preparations through increasingly hot PERi-PERi options.

The restaurant’s PERi-PERi sauces use ingredients including chilli, salt, garlic, lemon, onion, oil, and vinegar.

Do not assume every sauce or baste is nutritionally identical merely because it is used on the same chicken.

If NutritionSolver has a verified separate value for the chosen baste, apply it to the calculation.

If the published entrée nutrition already includes the restaurant’s standard preparation, do not add the same baste again.

Hotter Does Not Automatically Mean More Calories

Spice intensity and calorie content are different things.

A hotter PERi-PERi sauce does not automatically contain more calories than a milder one.

For example, current packaged Nando’s Hot and Garlic PERi-PERi sauces are only about 5 calories per teaspoon, while creamy PERinaise contains considerably more energy because it is a mayonnaise-style product.

Choose sauces according to their actual nutrition rather than ranking them by heat alone.

PERinaise Is Different From PERi-PERi Sauce

This distinction matters.

PERi-PERi sauces are chilli-based sauces.

PERinaise is a creamy PERi-PERi mayonnaise-style spread.

Nando’s current packaged Original PERinaise contains about:

45 calories per 3-teaspoon serving

with approximately:

4g fat

2g carbohydrates

190mg sodium .

That makes PERinaise nutritionally different from a small serving of standard hot PERi-PERi sauce.

If you add PERinaise to a sandwich, chips, or side, calculate it separately when it is not already included in the recipe.

Build the Nando’s Plate in the Right Order

Start with the exact chicken cut, wings, sandwich, wrap, bowl, or salad .

Then confirm the portion or piece count and your selected PERi-PERi preparation .

After that, add the exact Regular or Large sides , plus any PERinaise, PERi Ranch, hummus, extra sauce, or starter you consume.

Finally, account for a drink, dessert, or shared platter portion if it is part of your meal.

That ordering sequence gives a much more useful Nando’s nutrition result than relying on one generic calorie number for flame-grilled chicken.
