Blaze Pizza Calories Calculator

At Blaze Pizza, the calorie count starts with one important question: what exactly is on your pizza?

Crust, sauce, cheese, meats, vegetables, finishing drizzles, and the number of slices you eat all affect the final nutrition.

Use the Blaze Pizza Calories Calculator above to compare menu items, check calories and macros, and calculate the nutrition of the portion you actually eat.

Start With the Slice, Not the Whole Pizza

Blaze's standard 11-inch pizzas are cut into 6 slices , while Large pizzas are 14 inches and typically cut into 8 slices.

That distinction matters when reading nutrition information.

If a listed value represents 1 of 6 slices , eating three slices means you have eaten half of the 11-inch pizza. If you eat the entire pizza, the per-slice nutrition needs to be multiplied by six.

Large-pizza values should be calculated using their own serving information rather than assuming they scale exactly like the smaller pizza.

Always check whether the nutrition number refers to:

one slice, half a pizza, or the full pizza.

A Build Your Own Pizza Does Not Have One Calorie Count

There cannot be one accurate calorie number for every Blaze Build Your Own Pizza .

Your final pizza is the sum of its individual parts:

Crust + sauce + cheese + toppings + finishing sauces

A pizza with red sauce, mozzarella, grilled chicken, and vegetables will have a different nutrition profile from one built with multiple cheeses, pepperoni, meatballs, and an olive oil drizzle.

For a custom pizza, ingredient-level calculation is much more useful than assigning one generic calorie value to “Build Your Own.”

Crust Sets the Starting Point

Before adding toppings, choose the correct crust.

Blaze offers multiple crust options for its 11-inch pizzas, while its Large pizzas use Classic Dough.

Changing the crust can affect calories, carbohydrates, sodium, and the overall size of the finished pizza. This means the same toppings placed on two different crusts should not automatically produce the same nutrition total.

Start with the crust first, then build the rest of your pizza on top of it.

Toppings Add Up Differently

Not every topping changes pizza nutrition in the same way.

Vegetable toppings may add relatively few calories, while ingredients such as cheese, pepperoni, meatballs, chicken, bacon, olive oil, and finishing drizzles can have a larger impact.

A topping can also change nutrients beyond calories.

For example:

Meat toppings may increase protein, fat, and sodium.

Extra cheese can increase protein, fat, saturated fat, and sodium.

Vegetables may add smaller amounts of calories along with carbohydrates and fiber.

Finishing sauces and drizzles can add calories, sugar, fat, or sodium depending on the ingredient.

The most accurate approach is to calculate the pizza as it is actually built.

Signature Pizza or Build Your Own?

Blaze also offers Signature Pizzas such as Carnivore, Four Cheese, Herbivore, Meatball Pie, Blazed BBQ, and Spicy Pepperoni .

For these pizzas, use the nutrition entry for the standard recipe when you order it as listed.

Once you make significant substitutions, however, the standard nutrition number becomes less representative of the pizza you receive.

That is where ingredient-level calculation becomes more useful.

Take Two Changes the Portion

Blaze's Take Two menu combines half of an 11-inch pizza with another item such as a salad or Cheesy Bread option.

Half of an 11-inch pizza represents three of its six slices.

To understand the complete meal, calculate the pizza portion and then add the nutrition of the side you selected.

This is more accurate than using the nutrition for an entire pizza or looking only at the side.

Do Not Forget Cheesy Bread, Salads, and Desserts

Pizza may be the main reason for visiting Blaze, but the current menu also includes salads, Cheesy Bread, Pesto Garlic Cheesy Bread, desserts, drinks, and dressings .

If your order includes more than pizza, add those items separately.

For example:

3 pizza slices + side salad

Pizza + Cheesy Bread

Pizza + dessert + drink

can produce very different totals even when the pizza itself stays unchanged.
