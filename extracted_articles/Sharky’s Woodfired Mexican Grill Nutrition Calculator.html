Calculate Sharky’s in the Same Order the Meal Is Built

For a burrito , choose:

Recipe → Protein → Tortilla → Supported modifications

For tacos :

Taco style → Protein/preparation → Tortilla → Quantity → Sides if ordered as a plate

For a Power Plate :

Protein → Portion → Side 1 → Side 2 → additional side where applicable

For salads :

Full/Half → Protein → Dressing and modifications

Then add any:

queso, chips, guacamole, salsa, drink, dessert, or extra side .

For family or catering meals, calculate the entire order first and then estimate the amount personally eaten.
