Dutch Bros Nutrition Calculator

Dutch Bros drinks are highly customizable, which means the drink name alone does not tell you the full nutrition story.

A Golden Eagle can be hot or iced, made in different sizes, prepared with different milk, ordered with sugar-free flavors, or customized with Soft Top, whipped cream, drizzles, and other extras. Rebel energy drinks can be iced or blended, while lemonades, teas, smoothies, freezes, and seasonal drinks follow their own serving patterns.

Use the Dutch Bros Nutrition Calculator above to match the drink you actually order and compare calories, sugar, carbohydrates, protein, fat, sodium, and other available nutrition values.

Start With the Exact Drink Format

At Dutch Bros, preparation style can change nutrition even when the flavor name stays the same.

A drink may be:

Hot

Iced

Blended

Cold Brew

Nitro Cold Brew

Those versions should not automatically share one calorie number.

For example, a Rebel ordered iced can have a substantially different calorie and sugar total from the same flavor ordered blended.

Always match both the drink name and preparation style before comparing nutrition.

Size Can Change a Drink by Hundreds of Calories

Dutch Bros commonly offers Small, Medium, and Large sizes, and the difference is much more than extra ice or liquid.

Larger servings can contain more milk, flavor syrup, sauce, energy drink base, or blended mix.

This is particularly important for drinks such as:

Golden Eagle

Same drink + same preparation + different size

Golden Eagle Nutrition Depends on More Than Vanilla and Caramel

The Golden Eagle is one of Dutch Bros' signature drinks.

The standard version combines espresso, vanilla and caramel flavors with half-and-half and a caramel drizzle.

Current official nutrition data lists approximately:

Iced Small Golden Eagle: 320 calories

Iced Medium: 460 calories

Iced Large: 540 calories

Hot versions are different because their ingredient proportions differ.

If you switch from the standard drink to a Zero Sugar Added or Protein version, use that version's own nutrition instead of modifying the regular Golden Eagle manually.

Zero Sugar Added Does Not Mean Zero Calories

This distinction is particularly important at Dutch Bros.

Many Dutch Faves can be prepared with sugar-free syrups and sauces, including Golden Eagle, Caramelizer, Kicker, Cocomo, Annihilator, and other drinks.

However, a Zero Sugar Added coffee can still contain calories, carbohydrates, fat, and naturally occurring sugar from ingredients such as milk or half-and-half.

For example, the Golden Eagle Zero Sugar Added still uses a dairy base.

So:

Zero Sugar Added ≠ zero calories

and

Sugar-free flavor ≠ sugar-free entire drink

Use the complete drink nutrition rather than looking only at the syrup.

Rebel Energy Drinks Need Both Flavor and Style

Dutch Bros Rebel is its own major menu category.

The energy drink base can be combined with flavors such as Aftershock, Double Rainbro, Electric Berry, Shark Attack, Dragon Slayer, Peach Ring, and many others.

Rebels may be served iced or blended , and sugar-free versions are available for supported combinations.

The difference can be substantial.

A Plain Blended Rebel currently ranges from about:

200 calories, Small

300 calories, Medium

400 calories, Large

before additional flavor combinations are considered.

Flavored Rebels can contain considerably more sugar and calories depending on the recipe.

Sugar-Free Rebel Is a Different Product, Not Just Less Syrup

Dutch Bros also offers a sugar-free Rebel energy drink base.

That means some drinks can combine:

Sugar-free Rebel base + sugar-free flavors

for a much lower-sugar result than the standard version.

But the exact nutrition still depends on the flavor combination, size, preparation, and any additions such as Soft Top or floats.

Select the actual sugar-free Rebel entry instead of subtracting sugar from the standard version yourself.

Protein Coffee Needs Its Own Category

Dutch Bros now has a dedicated Protein Coffee menu.

Current options include drinks such as:

Golden Eagle Protein

Protein Latte

Protein Mocha

Vanilla Protein Latte

Hopscotch Protein Latte

and Zero Sugar Added Protein variations.

These drinks use Dutch Bros Protein Milk and can provide substantially more protein than a standard flavored coffee.

For example, current official nutrition lists an Iced Medium Golden Eagle Protein Latte at roughly 330 calories and 23g of protein .

That is a very different nutrition profile from the standard Iced Medium Golden Eagle.

Protein Coffee should therefore be treated as its own drink family, not simply another customization.

Soft Top Can Add More Than It Looks

Soft Top is one of Dutch Bros' most recognizable add-ons.

A standard serving currently contributes approximately 100 calories , along with fat, carbohydrates, and sugar.

That means:

Cold Brew

and

Cold Brew with Soft Top

are different nutrition entries.

The same applies when Soft Top is added to a Rebel, lemonade, protein coffee, seasonal drink, or another custom creation.

If it is already included in the standard recipe, do not add it twice. If you request extra Soft Top, count the additional serving separately.

Milk Choice Changes Coffee Nutrition

Dutch Bros drinks can be customized with different dairy and non-dairy milk options.

Switching between half-and-half, 2% milk, nonfat milk, chocolate milk, protein milk, oat milk, almond milk, or another available option can change:

Calories

Protein

Carbohydrates

Fat

Sugar

The impact is more noticeable in milk-heavy drinks than in an Americano with a small splash of milk.

When possible, calculate the exact milk used in your drink.

Cold Brew Can Start Simple and Become Much Richer

Plain Cold Brew and Nitro Cold Brew are relatively simple starting points.

From there, Dutch Bros can add cream, sweet cream, Soft Top, chocolate, caramel, flavored syrups, and other ingredients.

For example, a plain cold brew and a Golden Eagle Cold Brew are not nutritionally comparable just because both use cold brew coffee.

The flavor additions become part of the drink.

Lemonades Can Contain More Sugar Than Their Name Suggests

Dutch Bros lemonades combine its lemonade base with flavored syrups and can be ordered iced or blended.

Flavors such as Strawberry, Electric Berry, Palm Beach, Passion Fruit, and Tiger's Blood can contain substantial amounts of carbohydrates and sugar, particularly in larger sizes.

Some large flavored lemonades can exceed 100g of sugar.

For these drinks, size is especially important.

Do not use the nutrition for a Small iced lemonade when your actual order is a Large blended version.

Caffeine Is Another Useful Comparison

Calories and macros are only part of the Dutch Bros drink profile.

Coffee, Cold Brew, Nitro Cold Brew, Rebel energy drinks, chai, and certain teas can also contain caffeine.

The caffeine amount can change with the drink type and size.

Dutch Bros also allows many espresso drinks to be made with decaf espresso, while drinks such as lemonade, smoothies, shakes, and certain other beverages can be caffeine-free.

For users choosing between energy drinks and coffee, showing caffeine alongside calories and sugar would make the calculator much more useful.

Seasonal Drinks Need Frequent Updates

Dutch Bros rotates seasonal drinks more aggressively than many restaurant chains.

The current menu includes seasonal families such as Cosmic Cookie Dough , Stardust , and Supernova , spanning coffee, Protein Coffee, Rebels, lemonades, energy refreshers, and other drink types.

These drinks may disappear after a limited run.

NutritionSolver should clearly distinguish:

Current Seasonal Drinks

Historical or Discontinued Drinks

so an old nutrition record is not mistaken for something currently available.
