Nifty Fifty’s Nutrition Calculator

Nifty Fifty’s is built around the kind of meal where the extras can matter almost as much as the main item.

You might start with a freshly ground cheeseburger, add fresh-cut fries, order an onion ring or cheese topping, and finish with one of the restaurant’s hand-spun milkshakes. Another visit might mean a Philly cheesesteak, chicken sandwich, hot dog, breakfast platter, or a loaded order of smothered fries.

Use the Nifty Fifty’s Nutrition Calculator above to compare available calorie information and combine the individual foods you actually order.

Think About the Whole Diner Order

At Nifty Fifty’s, looking at the burger alone may tell only part of the story.

A typical order can include:

Burger or sandwich

Fresh-cut fries or onion rings

Cheese, bacon, chili, or other add-ons

Milkshake, malt, soda, or another fountain drink

The meal total can therefore be very different from the calorie value of the entrée.

For the most useful estimate, build the order one item at a time instead of stopping after the burger.

A useful Nifty Fifty’s calculator should eventually let users start with the base burger and apply the customizations they actually choose.

A Lettuce-Based Burger Is a Different Order

The current menu allows burger buns to be replaced with a bed of lettuce.

That changes the serving because the bread is removed while the beef and chosen toppings remain.

If verified ingredient nutrition becomes available, NutritionSolver should calculate this as a distinct customized burger rather than labeling it as identical to the standard sandwich.

It is also important not to turn a bun substitution into a broad allergen-safety claim. Preparation and cross-contact need separate consideration.
