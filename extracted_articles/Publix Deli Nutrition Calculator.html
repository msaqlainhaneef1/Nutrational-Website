Publix Deli Nutrition Calculator

Publix Deli nutrition is different from a typical sandwich restaurant because there is rarely just one way to order an item.

A turkey Pub Sub can use Publix turkey or Boar’s Head OvenGold turkey. You can order a Half or Whole sub, change the bread, switch the cheese, add double meat, remove mayonnaise, add avocado, choose a different sauce, or turn some sandwiches into wraps.

Then the Deli extends well beyond subs into chicken tenders, fried chicken, prepared meals, sliced meats and cheeses, Grab & Go foods, salads, soups, family meals, and party platters.

Use the Publix Deli Nutrition Calculator above to match the exact product and customization you actually order.

Start With the Serving Format

For a Publix sub, identify:

Half or Whole

before calculating anything else.

Publix currently sells many of its made-to-order subs in both portions.

The serving size matters because the bread, meat, cheese, toppings, and condiments all scale with the order.

A Half Turkey Sub should therefore never inherit the nutrition of a Whole Turkey Sub.

Bread Can Add Hundreds of Calories Before the Filling

Current Publix online product information lists whole-sub bread choices around:

White Sub Roll: approximately 470 calories

Whole Wheat Sub Roll: approximately 440 calories

Italian Five-Grain Sub Roll: approximately 540 calories.

Availability can vary by location.

The bread alone is therefore a substantial part of a Whole Pub Sub.

If you switch from one roll to another, NutritionSolver should adjust the bread component rather than keeping the original sandwich total unchanged.

Do Not Assume Wheat Is Always Available

Publix's current online ordering pages sometimes specifically note that wheat rolls are unavailable for the selected market.

This is important for the calculator.

The nutrition database can preserve the wheat option, but the page should not imply that every bread is available at every Publix store at all times.

Availability and nutrition are separate fields.

Publix Meat and Boar’s Head Meat Need Separate Records

Publix Deli sells its own sliced meats and Boar’s Head products.

Those should not be merged just because the meat type sounds similar.

For example:

Publix Oven Roasted Turkey

and:

Boar’s Head OvenGold Turkey

can have different serving nutrition and sodium.

The same distinction applies to ham, roast beef, and other deli meats.

A strong calculator should first ask:

Publix Deli or Boar’s Head?

and then apply the correct meat portion.

Build a Publix Deli Order From the Counter Forward

For a Pub Sub , choose:

Publix or Boar’s Head → Half or Whole → Bread → Meat → Cheese → Toppings → Condiments → Extras .

For chicken , choose the exact item and piece count, then add the selected sides, bread, and sauce.

For Grab & Go , use the packaged product's own serving information.

For family meals and platters , calculate either the entire package or the portion actually eaten.

Publix Deli has enough customization that one static calorie value is rarely the best answer. Matching the exact counter order produces a much more useful nutrition total.
