Why 7 Brew Calories Can Vary So Much

7 Brew is built around customization. The menu includes coffee, 7 Energy, Fizz sodas, teas, chai, matcha, lemonades, smoothies, shakes, and other drinks, with many options available in different sizes and preparations.

That creates wide calorie ranges even within the same drink family.

For example, changing any of the following can affect the final nutrition:

Drink size
A larger drink generally contains more ingredients and therefore more calories.

Milk or base
Breve, regular milk, oat milk, almond milk, coconut milk, and other options can produce different calorie, carbohydrate, and fat totals.

Flavor and sweetness
Syrups, sauces, drizzles, and sweetness levels can noticeably change carbohydrate and sugar content.

Hot, iced, or frozen preparation
Different versions of a drink may use different quantities or ingredients, so they should not automatically be treated as nutritionally identical.

Because of these differences, always select the closest match to your actual order.

What Does “Sugar-Free” Mean at 7 Brew?

7 Brew offers sugar-free flavors and a Sugar-Free 7 Energy option, but a sugar-free flavor does not necessarily make the entire finished beverage sugar-free .

Other ingredients in the drink, including milk or additional components, may still contribute naturally occurring or added sugars.

For the most accurate comparison, check the nutrition of the complete drink configuration , not just the flavor name.
