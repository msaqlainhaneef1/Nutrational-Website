Hawaiian Bros Nutrition Calculator

A Hawaiian Bros Plate Lunch is more than a serving of chicken or pork.

The standard meal combines your selected protein with white rice and macaroni salad, which means a large share of the meal's calories and carbohydrates can come from the sides as well as the entrée.

Use the Hawaiian Bros Nutrition Calculator above to compare Plate Lunches, proteins, sides, bowls, wraps, sauces, snacks, and desserts and see the nutrition of the complete meal you actually order.

Understand the Plate Lunch First

Hawaiian Bros builds its core menu around the Hawaiian-style Plate Lunch.

A standard Plate Lunch includes:

Your chosen protein + white rice + macaroni salad

Current protein choices include Huli Huli Chicken, Molokai Chicken, Honolulu Chicken, Kilauea Chicken, and Luau Pork.

That serving structure is important because the nutrition listed for a complete Plate Lunch should not be interpreted as the nutrition of the meat alone.

For example, the current Classic Huli Huli Chicken Plate in NutritionSolver contains approximately:

1,210 calories
42g protein
154g carbohydrates
46g fat
2,640mg sodium

Those numbers represent a full plate configuration rather than a simple serving of grilled chicken.

Huli Huli Chicken Is the Signature Starting Point

Huli Huli Chicken is Hawaiian Bros' signature teriyaki-style grilled chicken.

A Classic Huli Huli Plate currently contains around 1,210 calories and 42g of protein .

The protein itself contributes part of that total, but white rice, macaroni salad, and the sweet-and-savory preparation also contribute calories and carbohydrates.

If you change or remove the sides, your meal will no longer match the standard Classic Plate nutrition.

Luau Pork Has a Very Different Macro Profile

Luau Pork is slow-roasted pork seasoned with Hawaiian sea salt.

Its protein total is substantially higher than several of the chicken plates, but so are total calories and fat.

Rice Is a Major Part of the Plate

White rice is not a small garnish at Hawaiian Bros.

The restaurant describes the traditional Plate Lunch as including generous rice portions, which contribute a significant amount of carbohydrates to the finished meal.

If you eat less rice than the standard serving, your actual calorie and carbohydrate intake may be lower than the complete published plate.

If you order additional rice, add the extra serving separately.

Macaroni Salad Can Change the Plate More Than Expected

Hawaiian Bros macaroni salad is another substantial part of the standard Plate Lunch.

It is a creamy macaroni-based side rather than a simple vegetable salad.

That means it contributes carbohydrates and fat in addition to calories.

If you swap macaroni salad for seasoned vegetables or pineapple where the ordering system allows it, calculate the replacement rather than continuing to use the standard plate nutrition.

A Mixed Plate Needs Both Proteins

The current Hawaiian Bros menu allows customers to create a Mixed Plate by choosing two entrées.

This is especially useful if you want to combine flavors such as:

Huli Huli Chicken + Luau Pork

or

Molokai Chicken + Honolulu Chicken

A Mixed Plate should not be calculated using the nutrition for only one of those proteins.

The calculator should ideally use the actual portions of both selected entrées together with the sides included in the plate.

Build Your Own Bowl Works Differently

Hawaiian Bros now offers a Build Your Own Bowl .

The current restaurant menu lets you choose any Plate Lunch protein, serve it over rice, and select a topper such as:

Fresh pineapple

Macaroni salad

Seasoned vegetables

This creates a different serving structure from the traditional Plate Lunch.

A bowl with Huli Huli Chicken and pineapple should therefore not automatically inherit the nutrition for a Classic Huli Huli Plate with macaroni salad.

For a customized bowl, calculate:

Rice + selected protein + selected topper

Wraps Are Their Own Recipes

The current Hawaiian Bros menu also includes wraps such as:

Huli Huli Wrap

Molokai Mac Wrap

Pacific Island Wrap

These combine ingredients such as chicken, rice, pineapple, greens, macaroni salad, crunchy noodles, vinaigrette, and a large tortilla depending on the recipe.

The tortilla alone changes the meal structure substantially compared with a Plate Lunch.

Use the dedicated wrap nutrition rather than estimating it from a bowl or plate.

Pineapple Luau Sliders Need the Correct Quantity

Pineapple Luau Sliders combine slow-roasted pork with pineapple garlic sauce, spicy mango aioli, pickled red onions, and KING'S HAWAIIAN® slider buns.

Because sliders can be sold in different quantities or meal configurations, the number of sliders matters.

A single slider, multiple-slider snack, and slider meal should not share one nutrition total.

Count the quantity you actually eat and include any sides that come with the meal.

SPAM® Musubi Is More Than SPAM Alone

SPAM® Musubi combines seared SPAM with rice, teriyaki glaze, and seaweed.

The current NutritionSolver entry lists one serving at approximately:

330 calories

9g protein

42g carbohydrates

12g fat

Because rice and teriyaki glaze are part of the finished item, it should not be calculated from SPAM nutrition alone.

Treat the complete musubi as its own menu item.

Sauces Should Be Added When They Are Extra

Hawaiian Bros currently lists signature sauces including:

Teriyaki Sauce

Pineapple BBQ Sauce

Sauce can contribute additional calories, carbs, sugar, and sodium.

If the published plate already reflects the sauce used in the standard recipe, do not add it twice.

If you order an extra sauce cup, add that extra portion separately.

Dole Soft Serve® Belongs in the Meal Total

Hawaiian Bros also serves Pineapple Dole Soft Serve®.

The current NutritionSolver serving is approximately 180 calories with 42g of carbohydrates and 40g of sugar .

If dessert follows a Plate Lunch, it belongs in the complete meal calculation just like an extra side or beverage.

A 1,200-calorie plate and a 1,200-calorie plate plus dessert are two different orders nutritionally.

Sodium Is Important on These Plates

Several complete Hawaiian Bros Plate Lunches contain substantial sodium.

Current Classic entries include approximately:

Huli Huli Chicken: 2,640mg

Honolulu Chicken: 3,540mg

Molokai Chicken: 3,760mg

Kilauea Chicken: 3,820mg

Sauces and additional sides can raise the total further.

For a useful comparison, review sodium alongside calories and protein rather than looking only at the calorie number.

Calculate the Whole Hawaiian Bros Plate

Start by choosing the exact protein and serving format you order.

For a traditional Plate Lunch, make sure the calculation reflects the rice and macaroni salad or replacement sides . For a Mixed Plate, include both proteins. For a bowl, choose the actual topper. For wraps and sliders, use their dedicated recipes.

Finally, add any extra sides, SPAM® Musubi, sauces, drinks, or Dole Soft Serve® you consume.

That gives you a much more meaningful Hawaiian Bros nutrition total than looking at the chicken or pork alone.
