Compare Foods on the Same 100g Basis

Nutrition values can be difficult to compare when one food is listed by cup, another by slice, and another by serving.

NutritionSolver uses nutrition per 100 grams as a consistent reference point across the foods directory. This makes side-by-side comparisons easier because every food starts from the same amount.

For example, you can quickly compare how much protein, carbohydrate, or fat two foods provide per 100g instead of trying to convert different serving sizes yourself.

A 100g reference does not mean that 100g is the recommended serving size. It is simply a standardized way to present nutrition data.

Why Nutrition Values Can Vary

Nutrition facts for the same type of food are not always identical.

Natural foods can vary by variety, growing conditions, ripeness, cooking method, and water content. Prepared and packaged foods may also differ by recipe or formulation.

For that reason, the values shown here should be treated as reliable reference data rather than a guarantee that every individual portion will contain exactly the same amount.
