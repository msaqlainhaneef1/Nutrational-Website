QDOBA Nutrition Calculator

QDOBA gives you a lot of freedom to build an entrée, but that also means there is no single calorie number for a QDOBA burrito or bowl.

A bowl can start with rice and beans, then add Grilled Adobo Chicken, steak, carnitas, ground beef, queso, guacamole, salsa, cheese, sour cream, vegetables, or other toppings. A burrito can use many of those same ingredients while also adding a large flour tortilla. Nachos start with chips and queso before the protein and toppings are considered.

Use the QDOBA Nutrition Calculator above to build the exact order you eat and see the available calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, sodium, fiber, sugar, and other nutrition values.

At QDOBA, Free Does Not Mean Zero Calories

One of the most important QDOBA-specific details is its pricing policy.

QDOBA currently lets guests add the first portion of hand-crafted guacamole and 3-Cheese Queso at no extra charge on Create Your Own entrées . Extra portions can cost more.

That does not mean those ingredients are nutritionally free.

Choose the Entrée Format Before the Protein

QDOBA's current menu is organized around formats such as Bowls, Burritos, Quesadillas, Nachos, Tacos and Salads , alongside chips, dips, sides and chef-created Signature Eats.

The format matters because it determines what the meal starts with.

QDOBA's current official nutrition guide gives base menu-board ranges of approximately:

Burrito: 610 to 640 calories before the complete customization is considered.

Bowl: 310 to 330 calories.

Nachos: 730 to 740 calories.

Loaded Quesadilla: about 850 calories.

Cheese-Crusted Quesadilla: about 970 calories.

These figures describe standard base configurations and are not universal completed-entrée totals. QDOBA specifically notes that actual calories vary with ingredient choices and portions.

Black Beans and Pinto Beans Need Separate Records

Current standard servings are approximately:

Black Beans, 4 oz: 140 calories, 9g protein, 24g carbohydrates and 14g fiber.

A proper QDOBA calculator should let the customer choose Black or Pinto rather than treating all beans as one ingredient.

Build QDOBA in the Same Order You Order It

Start with the exact Bowl, Burrito, Taco, Quesadilla, Nachos or Salad .

Choose the appropriate tortilla or base , followed by rice, beans and protein .

Then add the actual queso, guacamole, cheese, sour cream, salsa, vegetables, crema and other toppings .

For current Signature Eats, use the verified complete recipe unless you modify it.

Finally, include chips, dips, Churro Chips, brownie, cookie or beverage if they are part of the order.

At QDOBA, the first serving of queso and guacamole may not change the price of a Create Your Own entrée, but they absolutely can change its nutrition.
