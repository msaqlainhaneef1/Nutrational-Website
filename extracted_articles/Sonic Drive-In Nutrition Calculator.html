Sonic Drive-In Nutrition Calculator

A Sonic order is not always one fixed meal.

You can choose a burger, chicken, hot dog, breakfast item, or snack, then add Tots or Groovy Fries and a drink. The drink itself may come in several sizes and can include fruit flavor, sweet cream, candy, lime, cherry, zero-sugar flavoring, or another add-in.

That means a useful Sonic nutrition calculator needs to handle both food combinations and drink customization .

Use the Sonic Drive-In Nutrition Calculator above to select the exact food, size, side, drink, and add-ins you actually order.

Sonic Nutrition Works Best as Two Connected Calculators

For food, the useful calculation is usually:

Entrée + side + sauce + drink

For Sonic drinks, it is:

Drink base + size + flavor/add-in + cream or topping

Those two systems should eventually connect into one complete meal total.

This matters because Sonic has one of the broadest beverage menus in fast food. Its current menu includes soft drinks, specialty sodas, slushes, cream slushes, iced tea, limeades, lemonades, cold brew coffee, and a new line of Refreshers.

Build a Sonic Order From the Drive-In Tray Up

For food, start with the exact burger, chicken item, hot dog, breakfast item, or snack .

Then select the correct size or piece count , followed by any supported additions or sauces.

Choose Tots, Groovy Fries, or another side and its actual size.

For drinks, select:

drink family → exact flavor → Wacky Pack/Small/Medium/Large/RT 44 where available → add-ins → cream/toppings .

Finally, add any shake, Blast, dessert, or extra snack consumed with the meal.

Sonic's food menu is broad, but its drink customization is what makes the restaurant especially different from most burger chains. A calculator that understands both systems can answer the actual order much more accurately than hundreds of disconnected nutrition cards.
