Potbelly Nutrition Calculator

At Potbelly, choosing the sandwich name is only the first step.

A Wreck, Italian, Turkey Breast, Avo Turkey, Chicken Club, Cubano, or another toasted sandwich can have very different nutrition depending on whether you order it as an Original, BIGS, Skinny, wrap, or another available format .

Then toppings, avocado, cheese, sauces, soup, Mac & Cheese, chips, cookies, or a hand-dipped shake can change the complete meal again.

Use the Potbelly Nutrition Calculator above to match the exact sandwich format and build the order you actually eat.

Choose the Potbelly Size Before Comparing Sandwiches

Potbelly does not use a simple Small, Medium and Large system for its signature sandwiches.

Its established sandwich formats include:

Original

BIGS

Skinny

Potbelly describes the Original as its regular sandwich size. BIGS uses a larger portion, while Skinny sandwiches use less meat and cheese and a reduced bread format.

The nutrition difference can be substantial.

Always select the format before relying on a calorie number.

BIGS Is More Than a Different Label

Meat

Cheese

Skinny Needs Its Own Published Nutrition

A Skinny sandwich is not simply half an Original sandwich.

Potbelly describes Skinnys as using less meat and cheese and a reduced bread format.

For example:

A Wreck Original: about 650 calories

A Wreck Skinny: about 440 calories.

The protein, carbohydrates, fat and sodium all change as well.

Use the actual Skinny record instead of mathematically shrinking the Original sandwich.

Build the Potbelly Meal Around the Serving Format

Start with the exact sandwich, wrap, salad, soup, Mac & Cheese, or breakfast item .

For sandwiches, choose:

Skinny → Original → BIGS

before changing bread, toppings, cheese or sauces.

For salads, select:

Half → Full

and add the correct dressing portion.

For soups and Mac & Cheese, choose the actual serving size.

For Pick-your-Pair , calculate both selected items together.

Finally, include any chips, side, cookie, shake, bottled drink or fountain beverage that is part of your order.

At Potbelly, identifying the serving format first is the simplest way to prevent one sandwich name from being mistaken for several very different nutrition totals.
