PDQ Nutrition Calculator

PDQ chicken nutrition depends on three decisions that can change the meal quickly:

How many pieces you order

How the chicken is prepared

Which sauce you add

A three-piece grilled tender order and a five-piece crispy order are not simply small and large versions of the same food. Add Garlic Parmesan, Hot Honey, Ranch, PDQ Sauce, waffle fries, or a shake, and the final meal changes again.

Use the PDQ Nutrition Calculator above to match your exact chicken preparation, piece count, sandwich, side, sauce, dessert, and drink.

The current menu lets customers choose tender and nugget preparations such as:

Piece count should therefore appear directly in every nutrition record.

Instead of:

PDQ Chicken Tenders

use:

PDQ Grilled Chicken Tenders, 3 Piece

or:

PDQ Crispy Chicken Tenders, 5 Piece .

This prevents a smaller portion from being mistaken for a larger meal.

Grilled and Crispy Chicken Need Different Nutrition

Preparation changes much more than appearance.

Grilled chicken does not use the same breaded coating as crispy chicken, while spicy crispy chicken has another preparation profile.

The current PDQ menu gives broad calorie ranges for three-, four-, and five-piece tender servings because the calories depend on the preparation selected.

For a three-piece serving, the current menu range is approximately 135 to 315 calories .

For four pieces, it is approximately 180 to 420 calories .

For five pieces, it is approximately 225 to 525 calories .

That is why the calculator should never apply one calorie number to all PDQ tenders.

Nuggets Need Their Own Piece Counts

12 Piece

The current menu gives approximately:

8 Piece: 120 to 180 calories

12 Piece: 280 to 420 calories

depending on the selected preparation.

A nugget order should therefore be calculated from:

piece count + cooking style

before dipping sauce is added.

Sauced Chicken Is a Different Order

PDQ also offers Sauced Tenders and Sauced Nuggets.

Current tossing sauce choices include:

Sweet Teriyaki

Hot Honey

Garlic Buffalo

Garlic Parmesan .

These should not simply inherit the nutrition of plain crispy chicken.

The chicken preparation is one component, and the tossing sauce changes the finished entrée.

A useful calculator should work as:

Choose chicken → choose piece count → choose preparation → choose tossing sauce

when a sauced version is ordered.

Tossing Sauce and Dipping Sauce Are Not the Same Thing

This distinction is especially important at PDQ.

A tossing sauce coats the chicken.

A dipping sauce is served separately and may only be partly consumed.

PDQ currently lists dipping sauces such as:

Buffalo

Ranch

Honey BBQ

Creamy Garlic

PDQ Sauce

Sweet Sriracha

Honey Mustard

Bleu Cheese .

Do not automatically add one full dipping sauce to every tender order unless that is what the customer actually consumes.

PDQ Sauce Can Add 150 Calories

The current PDQ menu lists approximately:

Buffalo: 80 calories

Honey BBQ: 80 calories

Ranch: 120 calories

Creamy Garlic: 140 calories

PDQ Sauce: 150 calories

Honey Mustard: 150 calories

Bleu Cheese: 150 calories

Sweet Sriracha: 160 calories.

That means sauce selection can change an order by around 80 calories before comparing portion sizes.

If you use two sauce cups, count two servings.

Sauce Calories Are Not the Only Thing That Matters

Sauces can also contribute substantial sodium, sugar, and fat.

A creamy sauce can add a relatively large amount of fat, while sweet sauces can add carbohydrates and sugar.

This is why PDQ nutrition should show more than calories.

Where available, compare:

Calories

Carbohydrates

Fat

Sodium

along with the chicken itself.

OG Classic and OG Deluxe Are Close, but Not Identical

The current PDQ menu lists approximately:

OG Classic: 695 calories

OG Deluxe: 700 calories.

The small calorie difference does not mean the products are duplicates.

The Deluxe includes additional vegetables and has a different finished recipe.

Keep both records searchable by their current names.

Spicy Classic and Spicy Deluxe Need Separate Records Too

Current values are approximately:

Spicy Classic: 695 calories

Spicy Deluxe: 700 calories.

These should not be hidden under one generic Spicy Chicken Sandwich row.

Users often search the exact named sandwich, and NutritionSolver should return that entity directly.

390 calories .

It should be a dedicated current record rather than grouped with fries or another starch.

Current PDQ serves Parmesan Broccoli , listed at approximately:

That is very different from plain steamed broccoli.

PDQ Salad Needs the Chicken Preparation

The current PDQ Salad combines mixed greens, red cabbage, carrots, grape tomatoes, cucumber, shredded cheese, and the customer's selected chicken preparation.

Current menu calories range approximately:

700 to 830 calories .

That range exists because Crispy, Spicy, and Grilled chicken do not create identical salads.

A good calculator should work as:

PDQ Salad → Grilled / Crispy / Spicy

and then apply the selected dressing.

Salad Dressing Should Not Be Hidden

Dressing can contribute a meaningful part of a salad's calories and fat.

If the published PDQ Salad value already includes a standard dressing, NutritionSolver should say so.

If dressing is separate, allow the customer to select the exact dressing.

Never silently include one dressing in some salads and exclude it from others.

Kids Meals Need Three Decisions

Current PDQ Kids' Meals combine:

Tenders or nuggets

Kids side

Kids drink .

Current kids-side options include foods such as fries, Parmesan Broccoli, and applesauce according to PDQ's April 2026 allergen guide.

That means one fixed Kids Meal calorie number is not enough.

The calculator should use:

Chicken choice → side → drink .

Build the PDQ Order by Piece, Preparation, and Sauce

Start with the exact tenders, nuggets, sandwich, or salad .

For tenders and nuggets, choose the correct piece count and whether they are Grilled, Crispy, Spicy, or Sauced .

Then add the exact tossing sauce or dipping sauce you consume.

Finally, include the Waffle Fries, Mac & Cheese, Parmesan Broccoli, Garlic Parm Toast, cookie, shake, lemonade, tea, or soda that is part of the meal.

That sequence matches how PDQ's current menu works and gives a much more useful nutrition total than using one generic chicken value.
