## What Are Macronutrients?

Macronutrients (often referred to simply as "macros") are the nutritional components that our bodies need in large amounts to function correctly, provide energy, and sustain life. The three primary macronutrients are:

1. **Protein**
2. **Carbohydrates**
3. **Fats**

Unlike micronutrients (vitamins and minerals), which are required in tiny doses, macronutrients provide the actual caloric energy our bodies run on.

---

## The Three Pillars of Nutrition

### 1. Protein: The Building Block

Protein is critical for growth, tissue repair, and immune function. It is composed of amino acids, which are often called the building blocks of life.

* **Energy Density:** 4 calories per gram.
* **Key Sources:** Chicken breast, eggs, fish, tofu, lentils, and lean beef.
* **Role:** Muscle recovery, enzyme production, and keeping you feeling full (satiety).

### 2. Carbohydrates: The Energy Provider

Carbohydrates are the body's primary and most efficient energy source. They are broken down into glucose, which fuels our muscles, brain, and central nervous system.

* **Energy Density:** 4 calories per gram.
* **Key Sources:** Whole grains, fruits (like apples), vegetables, oats, and potatoes.
* **Role:** Immediate energy, cellular fueling, and digestive health (via fiber).

### 3. Fats: The Hormone Regulator

Fats are essential for brain health, hormone production, and the absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (Vitamins A, D, E, and K).

* **Energy Density:** 9 calories per gram.
* **Key Sources:** Avocados, nuts, seeds, olive oil, and fatty fish.
* **Role:** Organ protection, cell membrane structure, and long-term energy storage.

## How to Calculate Your Macros

Calculating your daily macronutrient targets depends entirely on your **Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)** and your physical goals (e.g., losing weight or gaining muscle).

To structure a balanced baseline diet:

1. **Find your BMR** using our metabolic calculators.
2. **Multiply it** by your activity factor to get your TDEE.
3. **Apportion calories** to the three macronutrients based on your goals.

For example, a standard active lifestyle ratio might look like:

* **Protein:** 30% of daily calories
* **Carbohydrates:** 40% of daily calories
* **Fats:** 30% of daily calories
